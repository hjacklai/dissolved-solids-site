What the Dispatch is not

It is not a reservation reminder. If you have a booking with us, we will WhatsApp you separately. The Dispatch is for cocktail content, not transactional reminders.

It is not a discount engine. We do not run promotions through email. We do not have "newsletter-only" pricing.

It is not a sales funnel. Most issues do not even mention reserving. They are just cocktail writing. If you happen to come visit, that is a bonus.

It is not daily. Twelve issues a year, one per month, sent on a Friday morning around 10am KL time.

Why subscribe

If you genuinely enjoy reading about cocktails, want to expand your home bar knowledge, want one well-considered email a month rather than a flood, and want to know what is actually happening at small craft cocktail bars in PJ, this is for you.

If you are looking for daily content or promotional offers, this is not the right list.

How we handle your email

Stored only for sending the Dispatch. Never sold, shared, or rented. You can unsubscribe with one click at the bottom of any issue. Your address is deleted within 30 days of unsubscribing. Full details in our privacy policy.
