Cocktail bar near Bangsar.

Fifteen to eighteen minutes west of Bangsar by car. Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim and Soluble Solids in SS2. Quieter rooms, easier parking, the same quality of drinks.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Bangsar has more bars per square kilometre than any other Klang Valley neighbourhood. Telawi, Lucky Garden, Bangsar Village, the choices are not the problem. The problem on most Friday and Saturday nights is parking, crowding, and noise level. If you want a serious cocktail in a quieter room with much easier parking, the 15-minute drive west into PJ pays off. Here is what is over there.

Two cocktail bars in Petaling Jaya, both close to Bangsar

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11, 47400 PJ. 15 minutes from Bangsar Telawi off-peak. Maps · WhatsApp · Open Tue-Sun.

Soluble Solids, SS2. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24, 47300 PJ. 18 minutes from Bangsar Telawi off-peak. Maps · WhatsApp · Open Wed-Sun, 18:00 onwards.

The drive from Bangsar

From Bangsar Telawi or Lucky Garden, the route is straightforward: Jalan Bangsar to Jalan Maarof, then Lebuhraya Damansara-Puchong (LDP) heading north. Exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, continue south for SS2.

  • To Dissolved Solids: 15 minutes off-peak, 22 minutes during Friday peak.
  • To Soluble Solids: 18 minutes off-peak, 25 minutes during Friday peak.

By Grab: RM 18-25 to either bar from Bangsar Village.

The three honest comparisons with Bangsar

Parking. Bangsar parking is famously hard on Friday and Saturday after 20:00, circling for 20 minutes is normal. Our bars have free street parking after 18:00 with covered municipal lots within a 3-minute walk that almost always have space.

Noise level. Most Bangsar bars sit at conversation-difficult volume after 21:00. Both our rooms run the music well under conversation level. You can hear the person across the table.

Cocktail focus. Bangsar has a mix of cocktail-led, beer-led, and wine-led venues. The cocktail-specific bars are good but few. Both our outlets are cocktail-led exclusively; the spirit selection, glassware, ice, and bartender training reflect that.

What Bangsar has that we do not: walking-distance density of options (so if one room is full, the next is 50m away), street vibrancy, late-night supper after midnight. Each setting has trade-offs.

Which of our two suits the Bangsar mood

If you would normally go to Bangsar for a structured cocktail evening (printed list, classic drinks, comfortable seats, food on the side), Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim is the closer match. Opens earlier, more menu structure, snacks available.

If you would normally go to Bangsar for a long conversation-led night with friends, where you settle in for hours, Soluble Solids in SS2 is the closer match. No printed list, every drink custom, the bartender as the centre of the night.

Bangsar South too?

If you are coming from Bangsar South (KL Eco City, Vista Bangsar Avenue), drive times are similar: 17-20 minutes to either bar via the Federal Highway and LDP.
