Cocktail bar near Subang Jaya.

From Subang Jaya, USJ, Sunway, or SS15 to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 18 to 22 minutes by car. Serious drinks, easier parking, walk-ins welcome.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Subang Jaya has a strong dining scene but a lighter cocktail-bar scene than other parts of the Klang Valley. If you live in Subang, USJ, Sunway, or SS15 and want a serious cocktail evening, the 18 to 22 minute drive into central PJ pays off. Both of our outlets are easy to reach via the LDP or the Federal Highway.

The route

From Subang Jaya: take the LDP heading north. Exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, or continue further north and exit at Section 17 for Soluble Solids. The drive is mostly highway-grade road.

From Sunway/Bandar Sunway: take Persiaran Sultan Salahuddin Abdul Aziz Shah, join the LDP heading north. Same exits.

Why the trip into PJ is worth it

The honest comparison: Subang has plenty of casual bars and a couple of decent rooftop venues. What it has fewer of is dedicated cocktail bars that pour with bartender-grade attention to ingredient sourcing, ice, glassware, and balance.

Both our outlets sit firmly in that dedicated-cocktail-bar category. The trade-off for the 18-22 minute drive is a more focused drinking experience, easier parking, and rooms quiet enough to talk in.
