Cocktail bar near Subang Jaya.

From Subang Jaya, USJ, Sunway, or SS15 to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 18 to 22 minutes by car. Serious drinks, easier parking, walk-ins welcome.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Subang Jaya is one of the largest townships in the Klang Valley, stretching from SS12 through SS19 in the older core, out into USJ 1 to USJ 26 in the newer numbered subdivisions, and across to Bandar Sunway with its mall complex and universities. Strong dining scene, big student and young-professional population, but a lighter dedicated cocktail-bar scene than the neighbouring PJ corridor. If you live in Subang, USJ, Sunway, SS15, or Putra Heights and want a serious cocktail evening, the 18 to 22 minute drive north into central PJ is the short, easy answer.

The character of Subang Jaya

Subang has three distinct sub-areas. SS15, SS14, and SS12 are the older commercial-residential core, with hawker centres, the well-known SS15 mamak corner, and a dense network of student-driven cafés and casual bars around the Taylor's and Inti campuses. USJ is the newer numbered residential expansion to the west, mostly suburban with row commercial along the main roads. Bandar Sunway sits at the southwest, anchored by Sunway Pyramid, Sunway Lagoon, Sunway University, and the Sunway hotel cluster.

The drinking culture in Subang is heavily student-influenced and price-conscious. Casual bars and gastropubs in SS15 and USJ Taipan do well; the Sunway hotel rooftop bars pull a more upscale crowd; the small specialist cocktail-bar format with RM 38 to 48 cocktails is rarer here than in PJ proper. That said, there are some respectable cocktail rooms in Subang itself, particularly inside the larger hotel complexes.

The food scene is excellent. The SS15 hawker centre row, the USJ Taipan night-market stalls, the SS19 dim sum places, the Bandar Sunway chain restaurants, and a deep mamak rotation across most blocks. A typical Subang move is to eat local and drive north for the drink.

Why drive 20 minutes north

The PJ corridor (SS2, Damansara Kim, Section 17) sits one LDP exit away from Subang Jaya. From SS15 it is closer to drive to either of our bars than to most KLCC rooftop venues. The drive is mostly highway-grade and predictable.

What that 20 minutes buys you: a smaller room (50 to 60 seats) where the drink is the point rather than the soundtrack; a back bar with 60 to 100 bottles of real depth in vermouth, bitters, and amari; fresh-cut citrus prepped daily; a bartender who can taste mid-build and adjust. Easier parking than Sunway Pyramid on a Saturday night, and quieter rooms than the larger hotel bars.

The route

From Subang Jaya SS15 or USJ: take the LDP northbound. Exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, or continue one more interchange and exit at Section 17 for Soluble Solids. Mostly highway-grade road, predictable.

From Sunway / Bandar Sunway: join the LDP northbound via the Sunway interchange, same exits.

The LRT alternative: from USJ 7, USJ 21, Wawasan, Taipan, SS15, or Subang Jaya stations on the Kelana Jaya line, ride to Taman Bahagia. From there a 7 to 10 minute Grab to either bar. About 50 to 55 minutes door to door, useful if everyone in the group wants to drink.

What to order if it is your first time

At Dissolved Solids, two house signatures to start: the Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon, foamed) and the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan, lime, soda). Both demonstrate Malaysian-ingredient cocktails done with restraint and classical technique. If you would prefer something more classic, the Old Fashioned here is built right.

At Soluble Solids, skip the printed list (there is none). Tell the bartender three short answers: strength (light, medium, strong), spirit (gin, rum, whisky, mezcal, surprise), flavour direction (citrusy, herbal, smoky, sweet). The drink is built in front of you from the labelled infusion jars on the back wall.

Hours and when to come

For an early post-work drink (18:00 to 19:30), Dissolved Solids is the easier pick. For a longer settled evening, Soluble Solids is the better fit. Walk-ins are welcome at both. WhatsApp ahead for groups of six or more or for Friday/Saturday after 21:00.

Getting back to Subang after

The drive back via the LDP is fast post-midnight, 15 to 22 minutes with no traffic. Police checkpoints occasionally operate on the LDP southbound on Friday and Saturday nights; never drive after a drink. Grab is reliable for the return at 01:00 but surge-priced on weekends (budget RM 30 to 50 back).
