Thaipusam in PJ.

A respectful non-alcoholic cocktail-bar plan for Thaipusam in Petaling Jaya. NA Indian-coded mocktails, vegetarian-friendly snacks, considered service.

Thaipusam is the major Tamil Hindu observance honouring Lord Murugan. Across Malaysia, devotees fast, observe vows, and complete the kavadi procession (most famously at Batu Caves). Many devotees abstain from meat and alcohol during the days surrounding Thaipusam. At a craft cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya, the day calls for the NA programme: serious mocktails drawn from the Indian flavour palette, served with the same care as the regular menu.

The NA-focused approach

On Thaipusam, our NA programme is the default for guests observing or fasting. Same craft, same glassware. The alcoholic side remains available for guests who are not observing.

What to order on Thaipusam

The drinks lean Indian-coded, cooling, and clarifying. Many guests come to the bar after long hours of walking, processing, or hosting family.

Cardamom and Rose Cooler: cardamom syrup, rose syrup, lemon, soda.

Tamarind and Jaggery Sour: tamarind pulp, jaggery syrup, lemon, ice. Refreshing, deeply Indian.

Mango Lassi: fresh mango, plain yogurt, cardamom, sugar.

Rose Lassi: rose syrup, plain yogurt, cardamom.

NA Masala Chai Espresso: proper masala chai, cooled, with NA espresso-style coffee.

Iced Masala Chai: the brewed spice mix with jaggery and milk.

Sandalwood Lemonade: sandalwood-infused syrup (food-grade, small quantity), lemon, soda.

Saffron Honey Cooler: saffron syrup, honey, lemon, soda.

Vegetarian-friendly snacks

Our regular bar snacks are mostly vegetarian (olives, salted nuts, popcorn, cheese). On Thaipusam we can hold the cheese and bring out a small fully-vegetarian board: olives, nuts, dried fruit, dosa-style crackers. Tell the bartender on arrival if you would like the vegetarian snack pairing.

The Thaipusam evening plan

Morning to afternoon: the procession, the kavadi, the temple visits.

Evening, 7pm-9pm: a quiet sit at the bar with NA cocktails, lassi, and masala chai. The cocktail bar is for after the day's intensity.

Our two PJ outlets

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; music softer on Thaipusam.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, NA programme as default on the night.
