Anniversary cocktail bar in PJ.

A quieter, more flexible alternative to the fixed-menu anniversary dinner. How to brief a small PJ bar in advance, what to order, and the room qualities that mark the year without making a scene.

Anniversaries are an event for two people, not for the room. The fancy restaurant tradition tries to package it: set menu, candles on every table, a waiter-led tribute that interrupts your conversation. A small Petaling Jaya bar lets you do the opposite: quiet table, the drink that means something to both of you, no announcement to the room. The marking is private.

Why PJ small bars handle anniversaries well

Smaller crowds. The room is not 80 percent full. Your table is not three feet from the next one. The conversation stays yours.

Briefable bartenders. WhatsApp a small PJ bar 24 hours ahead, tell them you are coming for an anniversary, and the bartender will prep. Tell them what you both drink, what your partner would love, and they will make sure the seat is right and the drinks are calibrated.

Flexibility on the night. The bar is not a kitchen with a service schedule. If you want to stay 90 minutes or four hours, both work. If you want a small bar snack and not a meal, that works.

The right kind of "marking". Quiet, attentive service. The bartender will quietly upgrade the garnish, write the year on a small card, or pour the drink in the better glassware. Without theatre.

How to brief us in advance

For an anniversary, WhatsApp the bar 24-72 hours ahead. Useful information to send:

  • Date and time you are arriving.
  • Anniversary year number (we can fold it into the drink or just acknowledge it on the seat).
  • What you usually drink.
  • What your partner usually drinks. What they would never drink.
  • Any "first drink we shared" or "what we ordered on the wedding day" we can replicate.
  • Whether you want the table or counter.
  • Whether it is a surprise (so we know not to announce it to the room).

We will respond within an hour confirming the prep.

What to order on an anniversary

Anniversaries reward continuity. If you have a meaningful drink from a past anniversary, dating period, or your wedding, order it again.

If you do not have a sentimental anchor:

French 75: gin, lemon, sugar, champagne. The anniversary classic. Celebratory but small.

Old Fashioned: bourbon, sugar, aromatic bitters. The "we have been together long enough to drink grown-up drinks" choice.

Scented Negroni: our house signature, floral-aromatic. Recipe.

The Tesseract: our other signature, four flavour dimensions in one glass. For curious palates. Recipe.

Gula Melaka Old Fashioned: Malaysian-local, slow and dark. Recipe.

Bellini: peach puree and prosecco. Floral, gentle.

For a non-drinking partner

The NA programme handles anniversaries the same way: same care, same glassware. Drinks worth specifying in advance:

  • NA French 75: NA gin substitute, lemon, sugar, NA sparkling.
  • NA Negroni or NA Old Fashioned.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • Rose Lychee Cooler.

Our two PJ outlets

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. Table-for-two seats along the wall. Warm lighting. We can put a small candle on the table on request.

Soluble Solids · SS2: the smaller of the two. No printed menu. Best when you brief us in advance with the drinks you want and what they should evoke.

The PJ anniversary evening plan

6:30pm-8:30pm, dinner: a restaurant you both love. Eat first; the bar is the second act.

9pm, opening drink: table-for-two, the meaningful drink (or the modern equivalent).

10pm-11pm, second drink: the slower, stronger choice. Old Fashioned or Negroni-coded.

11pm, optional digestif: a cognac, a port, a small dessert cocktail. Then the trip home, with the anniversary properly marked.
