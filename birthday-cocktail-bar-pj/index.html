Birthday cocktail bar in PJ.

A quieter, more deliberate birthday at a small Petaling Jaya cocktail bar. How to brief in advance, the custom-drink move, and ordering when the group is mixed-drinker.

A PJ birthday is the calmer version of the KL birthday. Smaller rooms, attentive bartender, no rooftop spectacle, the room actually quiet enough that the birthday person can hear the people who came to celebrate them. For close-friend birthdays (4-12 people), PJ is the better option. For big group birthdays (20+), KL or a buyout.

The PJ small-bar birthday format

The format that works best at a small PJ craft bar:

  • 4-8 of the birthday person's closest friends or family.
  • Dinner first at a restaurant nearby.
  • The bar from 9pm to midnight.
  • Two or three rounds, paced.
  • A custom drink built by the bartender for the birthday person.
  • No public spectacle, no waiter-singing.
  • Cake brought from a bakery (optional).

Brief us in advance

For a birthday, WhatsApp 5-7 days ahead. Useful information:

  • Date, arrival time, group size.
  • Whose birthday and their usual drink order.
  • Their favourite spirit, favourite flavour, hated flavour.
  • Anyone in the group with allergies or non-drinkers.
  • Whether it is a surprise.
  • Whether you want a custom drink built for them.
  • If you are bringing a cake or want us to handle the dessert moment.

We will reply within an hour with confirmation and any logistics (e.g. "if you are bringing the cake, please drop it off by 7pm so we can refrigerate it").

The custom-drink move

This is the move that makes a small-bar birthday memorable: ask the bartender to build a one-off cocktail for the birthday person, briefed in advance.

The brief: their favourite spirit, a flavour they love, a flavour they hate, and any past drink they have ever loved (and where they had it). The bartender will build a custom drink, sometimes named for the night, sometimes for the person. It costs the same as a regular cocktail.

The drink lands at the table on round one, with a small recipe card and the bartender's small explanation. The rest of the table orders normally; the birthday person gets the custom drink.

What the group orders

The custom drink is for the birthday person on round one. The rest of the table orders normally. By round two, the table has often identified 2-3 drinks people are gravitating to; order in clusters.

Birthday-coded drinks worth ordering for the group:

French 75 (the celebratory classic). Negroni Sbagliato (Campari + sweet vermouth + prosecco). Espresso Martini (the "everyone wants one" drink for late evening). The Tesseract and the Scented Negroni (our house signatures, for the curious in the group).

For non-drinkers in the group

The NA programme runs in parallel. Drinks worth specifying:

  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey (espresso + palm sugar), Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • NA spritzes: elderflower, hibiscus.
  • NA Negroni or NA Old Fashioned.
  • Brewed tea cocktails: osmanthus, white tea, jasmine.

Our two PJ outlets, for birthdays

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. Groups of 6-12 fit on the long table or by combining bar-counter sections. The room is warm-lit and conversation-volume.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, best for 4-8 person birthdays. The bespoke build format suits the custom-drink move particularly well.

The PJ birthday evening plan

Dinner first, 7pm-9pm: a restaurant nearby. PJ has good Chinese, Italian, Japanese, and Malaysian dining options across the SS2, Damansara Kim, and Damansara Heights neighbourhoods.

Bar, 9:30pm-midnight: three rounds. Round one is the custom drink for the birthday person + everyone's first drink. Round two is the cluster round. Round three is the wind-down.

Cake moment (optional): we bring the cake out around round two, with candles. Or skip it entirely.
