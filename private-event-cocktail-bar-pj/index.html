Private event cocktail bar in PJ.

Renting a small craft cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya for a private event. How buyouts work at our two outlets, what they include, and how to brief the bar 3-6 weeks ahead.

PJ is the right scale for intimate private events. The small bars here hold 20-40 guests comfortably, which is the most common buyout size in the Klang Valley. The bartender can dedicate full attention. The drinks stay at craft standard. The bill is cleaner than the equivalent KL central spend. The trade-off: you do not get the rooftop view, but you do get an actual conversation across the room.

What we mean by "private event"

Anything from 15-40 guests where you want the room to yourselves and the bar to make the night specific to your group. Common formats:

  • A milestone birthday (30, 40, 50, 60).
  • A wedding rehearsal dinner or "thank you to the family" night.
  • A team year-end or "thanks team" event for 15-35 people.
  • An engagement party, an anniversary, a baby shower with drinks.
  • A product launch, brand activation, or small media event.
  • A book launch, exhibition opening, or creative gathering.

Our two PJ outlets, for buyouts

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. Seated capacity 30, standing capacity ~40. Best for buyouts of 18-35 guests. Warm-lit, conversation-volume, with a long table and bar-counter sections that can be configured for the night.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, on Jalan SS2/24. Seated capacity 20, standing ~25. Best for buyouts of 12-22 guests. The bespoke-build format makes this outlet feel particularly tailored when bought out for a private night.

What a buyout includes

  • Exclusive use of the room for the agreed window (typically 4-5 hours).
  • The full cocktail programme available, plus any custom drinks briefed in advance.
  • Dedicated bartender(s) for the group.
  • Music programming coordinated to the event. We can run our usual or fit a specific brief.
  • Bar snacks at the regular menu, or a catered spread arranged through one of our partner kitchens for additional charge.
  • Printed cocktail menu for the night, named for the event if you want.
  • Small branded materials on request (custom napkins, recipe cards, small print items).

The pricing structure

We use a minimum spend, not a flat rental fee. The minimum reflects the regular revenue we would expect for that night and outlet. If the guests order enough drinks to hit it, no further fee. If not, the difference is charged.

Minimums vary by:

  • Night of the week. Weekend buyouts (Fri-Sat) cost more than weeknight buyouts (Tue-Thu).
  • Outlet. Dissolved Solids (larger room, larger capacity) carries a higher minimum than Soluble Solids.
  • Window length. A 4-hour buyout costs less than a 6-hour buyout.
  • Catering. If we coordinate food, that adds.

The custom drink menu

For private events we typically build a 4-6 drink menu tailored to the night. The brief includes:

  • The kind of evening (warm and personal, professional, celebratory, somber).
  • Flavours or ingredients to feature (rose, gula melaka, cardamom, mezcal, sake).
  • Anything to avoid (allergies, the host's hated flavour, alcohol categories someone in the group cannot drink).
  • A signature drink for the host or honouree, if applicable.
  • NA versions of each cocktail in the menu (not optional; we include them by default).

The menu is named for the event and printed on small recipe cards. The bartender opens the night by presenting it to your guests.

This costs nothing extra but requires 2-3 weeks lead time to build and trial.

How to brief us

WhatsApp 3-6 weeks ahead with:

  • Date, start time, expected end time.
  • Group size and likely range (e.g. "25 confirmed, possibly up to 32").
  • Event type and feel.
  • Whether food is needed and the format (light canapes, full sit-down dinner via catering).
  • Whether you want a custom menu or branded drinks.
  • Music preferences.
  • "Must-have" guest names, allergies, non-drinkers.
  • Budget range, or whether you want us to quote against the minimum spend.

We will reply within 24 hours with a proposed format, the minimum spend, confirmation of availability, and any follow-up questions.
