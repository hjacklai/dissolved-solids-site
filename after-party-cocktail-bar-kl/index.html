After party venue near KL.

An intimate late-night cocktail bar a short Grab from Kuala Lumpur for a wedding or event after party. Buyout or partial booking, open till 01:00 on weekends, custom drinks, 12 to 40 people.

From 12 to 40 guests·20 to 25 min from KL

The after party is the part of the night the venue never quite gets right. A KL hotel ballroom empties out, the lights come up, and the people who actually want one more drink end up in the hotel lobby bar paying corkage prices for a tired pour. A small cocktail bar is the better answer, and you do not have to stay in the city for it. Our two rooms sit just over the border in Petaling Jaya, 20 to 25 minutes from KLCC, Bangsar or Mont Kiara by Grab off-peak. They hold 12 to 40 guests, run until 01:00 on the late nights, and pour at craft standard while the dinner crowd thins to the people who came to talk. The bill runs 20 to 30% cleaner than a KL hotel-bar after party, and the room feels like the natural end of the night rather than a second venue.

What we mean by after party

Anything that follows a main event and wants a smaller, warmer, later room:

  • Wedding after party. Post-dinner, post-reception. The couple and the people who are not ready to go home. A signature drink named for the couple, lighter at first, then settling into the long pours.
  • Event or gala after party. The close of a corporate dinner, an awards night, a conference. The room where the real conversation happens once the programme ends.
  • Birthday or milestone after party. The dinner was somewhere else; the drinks are here.
  • Launch or screening after party. A quieter, more curated end than a club, with a drink tied to the work.

What an after party buyout includes

  • Exclusive use of the room for the agreed late window. No walk-ins during your window.
  • The full cocktail programme plus any custom or signature drinks briefed ahead.
  • Dedicated bartender(s) scaled to the group.
  • Music programming tuned to the after-party arc: settling, not building to a club peak.
  • Bar snacks, or a catered spread arranged through a partner kitchen for an additional charge. Outside food and cake are welcome.
  • A non-alcoholic version of every cocktail, included by default, for guests who are driving back to KL late.

What it costs

A minimum spend, not a flat fee. Guest drinks count toward it.

  • Weeknight (Tue to Thu), 15 to 30 guests: RM 3,500 to RM 6,000 minimum spend.
  • Friday or Saturday, 25 to 40 guests: RM 6,500 to RM 9,500 minimum spend.
  • Partial-section booking for 12 to 20 guests: smaller minimum, ask for a quote.

These rates run roughly 20 to 30% below an equivalent KL hotel-bar after party for the same group, and the Grab back into the city is short.
