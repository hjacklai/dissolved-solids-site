A KLCC hotel function room is the safe corporate choice and the forgettable one. A small craft cocktail bar is the one people actually talk about the next morning. Team-building cocktail sessions

Our most popular corporate add-on. The bartender teaches the group two or three drinks, then each person builds their own; we can run it as a friendly competition with the team voting a winner. It runs 60 to 90 minutes for 10 to 30 people and pairs naturally with a dinner or a drinks reception afterward. Non-alcoholic builds are included by default, so the whole team takes part regardless of who is drinking. See our cocktail workshop page for the hands-on format.

Company billing, done properly

Corporate bookings need clean paperwork, so we keep it simple:

  • Tax invoice in your registered company name, with SST shown, suitable for expense claims and finance records.
  • Payment by company bank transfer, card, or DuitNow. A deposit (usually 30 percent) confirms the date; the balance settles on or after the night against the invoice.
  • PO and billing details captured up front. Send the company name, registration number if needed, and any PO reference when you book.
  • A single point of contact from us through the whole booking, so finance and the organiser are not chasing different people.

Speeches, awards, and a screen

We have a small PA and microphone for a toast, a short speech, or an awards moment, and a screen can be arranged for a brief slide or a product reveal. The rooms are built for speeches and launches rather than full conference AV. Tell us the run-of-show and we set the room and the music to match it. Larger production (DJ, full projection, stage) is coordinated externally with enough notice.

What it costs

A minimum spend, not a flat venue fee. Guest drinks count toward it, so a well-attended evening usually meets the minimum naturally.

  • Weeknight (Tue to Thu), 15 to 30 guests: RM 3,500 to RM 7,000 minimum spend.
  • Friday or Saturday, 25 to 40 guests: RM 7,000 to RM 12,000 minimum spend.
  • Team-building session: quoted on top, based on group size and duration.

Our rates run roughly 20 to 30 percent below an equivalent KLCC hotel function room for the same group, and parking does not add a line to anyone's claim.
