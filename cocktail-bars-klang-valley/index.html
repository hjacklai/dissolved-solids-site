Cocktail bars in the Klang Valley.

Both our cocktail bars sit in the Klang Valley, in Petaling Jaya. Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2. Drive times from KL, Mont Kiara, Bangsar, Subang Jaya, Puchong, Cheras.

The Klang Valley covers a lot. Kuala Lumpur city centre, the PJ corridor, Mont Kiara, Bangsar, Subang Jaya, Shah Alam, Puchong, Cheras, Cyberjaya. Our two cocktail bars sit in Petaling Jaya, near the geographic centre of the Klang Valley. From most of those neighbourhoods, we are a 15 to 30 minute drive off-peak.

By public transport

LRT. Closest stops to either bar are Taman Bahagia (then 5-7 minute Grab) or Bandar Utama (then 7-10 minute Grab).

MRT. The MRT2 (Putrajaya Line) does not yet reach the PJ cocktail district conveniently. From KL Sentral or KLCC, Grab is faster than MRT for both bars.

Grab. RM 18 to 30 from most KL neighbourhoods, off-peak. Surge to RM 35 to 55 on Friday and Saturday after 22:00.

Why drink in PJ if you live in KL

Three reasons. First, PJ has matured fast as a cocktail district — the rent is cheaper than KLCC, which lets owner-operated bars invest more in spirit, glassware, and bartender training. Second, parking is genuinely easier (street parking is free after 18:00 in most PJ commercial zones, vs RM 5+/hour in KLCC). Third, the rooms here are smaller and quieter on average than the loud rooftop scene downtown. Conversation actually possible.

That said: KL has plenty of good cocktail bars too. We are not claiming to be the only option — just that the 20-minute drive west often pays off.
