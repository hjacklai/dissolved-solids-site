Asia's 50 Best Bars Malaysia.

The Malaysian cocktail bar award landscape. Asia's 50 Best Bars 2025, Tatler Asia Best 20 Bars Malaysia 2025 and 2026. The full Malaysian map.

The Malaysian cocktail bar scene has had a breakthrough decade. 2025 was the year Malaysia secured four bars in the Asia's 50 Best Bars top-50 for the first time. Tatler Asia's Best 20 Bars Malaysia (2025 and 2026 editions) added domestic granularity. This page is a working map of the Malaysian award landscape, plus where our two outlets sit.

Asia's 50 Best Bars 2025, Malaysian entries

The Asia's 50 Best Bars list is the regional ranking equivalent of the World's 50 Best. The 2025 edition was a breakthrough year for Malaysia with four bars in the top 50:

  • Penrose · #10 (best in Malaysia): Kuala Lumpur. Cocktail bar with chef-led food programme. Previously ranked in the World's 50 Best.
  • Three X Co. · #15: Bukit Bintang, KL. Pre-Prohibition aesthetic with serious craft cocktail programme.
  • Bar Trigona · #39: Four Seasons Hotel KL. The honey-bee-themed bar by Ashish Sharma.
  • Reka · #47: Kuala Lumpur. Modern bar focused on Malaysian flavour identity in cocktails.

For context, Penrose has been a regular Asia's 50 Best entrant since 2022, and was a 2024 World's 50 Best entrant. Three X Co. and Bar Trigona have rotated in and out of the top 50 over the past 3-4 years.

Tatler Asia Best 20 Bars Malaysia 2026

Tatler's Malaysian list is more granular and includes bars outside the KL central business district. The 2026 edition (published March 2026) included:

  • The major KL bars from the Asia's 50 Best list (Penrose, Three X Co., Bar Trigona, Reka).
  • JungleBird (KL, 1978 Hilton legacy + modern craft).
  • Coley (Bangsar, classic American cocktail bar).
  • Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim, PJ), #9.
  • Other rotating PJ and KL entries.

The Tatler list distinguishes itself from Asia's 50 Best by including PJ and Klang Valley bars that the Asia-wide list typically misses. For a working PJ + KL cocktail bar list, Tatler is the more useful starting point.

Tatler Asia Best 20 Bars Malaysia 2025

The 2025 edition (published April 2025) had similar major entries:

  • Penrose, Three X Co., Bar Trigona, JungleBird, Reka.
  • Dissolved Solids, #6.
  • Other rotating PJ and KL entries.

Both Tatler lists describe Dissolved Solids as a "hybrid bar" offering cocktails, mocktails, coffee, and tea, with the team's signature being a customisable approach to every drink.

The award ecosystem context

Three Malaysian bar awards matter:

  • World's 50 Best Bars / Asia's 50 Best Bars: international. Reach: global. Major recognition driver. Malaysian entries: 4 in 2025 (top 50).
  • Tatler Asia Best 20 Bars Malaysia: regional, Malaysia-specific. Reach: domestic + regional Tatler audience. Annual list, two-year history.
  • Time Out KL / KL Foodie / MONO Malaysia / Eat Drink KL: local food-media listicles. Reach: Klang Valley readership. Less prestigious but high local-search visibility.

Our positioning

Dissolved Solids has been listed in Tatler Asia's Best 20 Bars Malaysia in both 2025 (#6) and 2026 (#9). We have not been on Asia's 50 Best Bars (the international list); the criteria there lean towards KL central-business-district venues with significant marketing scale.

Honest read: we are a strong domestic-recognised cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya, the sister bar Soluble Solids in SS2 less so. Both run small-format craft cocktail programmes. Tatler is the right reference frame for us; Asia's 50 Best is a different category of venue scale.
