## Part 4 - Sitemap freshness

```

  EN URLs:                          204

```

**The big one: 203 EN production pages are missing from `sitemap.xml`.** This means roughly 50% of the EN site is invisible to Google's crawl prioritisation, and the ZH versions are present but their EN canonicals are not. Categories most affected:

- Ingredients hub + 50 ingredient pages (all 50 ingredient pages missing)
- 33 of 149 journal articles (newer ones, mostly from Rounds 19+)
- 21 cocktail recipes (newer signature + recent additions)
- All 60+ intent/festivity landers (Rounds 23-29)
- `cheatsheets/`, `calculators/`, `story/`, `press/`, `newsletter/`, `cocktail-workshop-kl/`, `asia-50-best-bars-malaysia/`, `best-cocktails-kl/`, `best-espresso-martini-kl/`, `best-negroni-kl/`, `best-old-fashioned-kl/`, `what-can-i-make/`, `speakeasy-pj/`, `happy-hour-pj/`, all `after-work-drinks-*`, all `anniversary-cocktail-bar-*`, all `birthday-cocktail-bar-*`, all `date-night-*`, all `first-date-*`, all `mothers-day-*`, all `fathers-day-*`, all `valentines-day-*`, every festivity lander (Chap Goh Mei, Deepavali, Easter, Halloween, Hari Raya, Israk Mikraj, Labour Day, Malaysia Day, Maulidur Rasul, Merdeka, Mid-Autumn, Ponggal, Pride, Teachers' Day, Thaipusam, Thanksgiving, Wesak, Women's Day, Awal Muharram, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Valentine's Day).

The ZH counterparts of most of these pages ARE in the sitemap, which is even more peculiar: Google sees ZH pages but no EN canonical exists in the sitemap to anchor them, weakening hreflang clustering.

The sitemap also has the xhtml:link hreflang annotation pattern only on the 397 ZH-bearing URL blocks (because each ZH block carries `<xhtml:link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-MY">`). EN-only blocks have no hreflang annotation. With ZH ahead of EN in URL coverage, Google's hreflang clustering is asymmetric.

**Fix:** A single sitemap rebuild that walks the `find . -name "index.html"` output would close this gap. The current sitemap looks frozen from an earlier content cycle (last-mod dates max out at 2026-05-16; subsequent rounds have not been added).

## Part 5 - Honest summary

### What the site is doing exceptionally well

1. **Content depth at scale.** 805 production pages, every single recipe/ingredient/journal page above its category threshold in both languages. Median ZH journal article is 2,381 chars (roughly 1,200 to 1,500 word equivalent in English). Median EN journal article runs comfortably above 1,500 words. This is rare for a two-outlet hospitality site.
2. **Schema-rich structured data.** 99.5% of production pages have at least one JSON-LD block. FAQPage schema is on every EN recipe (94), every EN ingredient (50), every EN journal (149), and 81 of 90 EN landers. Breadcrumbs, BarOrPub, Recipe, and HowTo schemas are also widely deployed (visible from spot checks).
3. **Comprehensive intent coverage.** All 30 of the high-intent queries audited resolve to a dedicated, deep, FAQ-schema'd page. The festivity coverage (16 Malaysian holidays plus Western secular events, both KL and PJ versions) is broader than any competing bar site in the region.
4. **Bilingual at parity.** 397 ZH pages mirror 408 EN, with hreflang on the deep tree, no thin ZH pages, FAQ translations across recipes/ingredients/journal. Almost no other Malaysian F&B site is bilingual at this depth.
5. **Forbidden-pattern hygiene.** 1 em-dash and 0 user-visible "DSSS" abbreviations across 805 pages is excellent enforcement of the writing rules.

### Where improvement would lift ranking

1. **Sitemap is stale by 203 EN URLs.** Half the EN site is not in `sitemap.xml`. Rebuild from disk and resubmit in Search Console. This is the single highest-leverage fix on the site and is purely mechanical. Without sitemap presence, Google relies on internal-link discovery plus crawl budget, which slows indexing of every new page since the last sitemap refresh.
2. **ZH lander FAQ schema gap.** 81 of 83 ZH landers have no FAQPage schema. The ZH FAQ blocks need to be ported (Round 36 did this for recipes/ingredients/journal but not landers). Direct impact on ZH SERP rich results.
3. **Bilingual hreflang missing on the 3 most important pages.** Root `/`, `/dissolvedsolids/`, `/solublesolids/` lack proper `<link rel="alternate" hreflang>` tags. These are the highest-PR pages on the site; the rest of the tree is correct. Add proper hreflang to these three pages.
4. **Title length cleanup on 161 EN pages.** Trim to roughly 60 to 65 chars by dropping the redundant `· Dissolved Solids` suffix where the body of the title already names the brand or where the eyebrow plus h1 cover it. Easy mass-edit. Specifically the journal long-form titles are the worst offenders.
5. **Meta description length cleanup on 142 EN pages.** Trim to 150 to 155 chars. The top 5 (homepage, both brand pages, ingredients hub, cocktails hub) are 280 to 294 chars; more than half the visible CTR copy is being clipped on mobile SERPs.
6. **7 broken internal links.** Mechanical fix (one hour of work). The `/cocktails/the-tesseract/` slug error appears on 5 neighbourhood landers and is the single most embarrassing miss because Tesseract is the signature drink being cross-promoted.
7. **`reserve/index.html` has no JSON-LD.** Add `WebPage` plus `ContactPoint` for both bars' WhatsApp numbers plus a `LocalBusiness` action. This is the conversion endpoint and is currently invisible to schema parsers.

### What is NOT in this audit (and matters for ranking)

On-page is necessary but not sufficient. The following are off-page factors I cannot verify from the file tree, and they materially affect actual Google rankings:

- **Domain authority and backlink profile.** Whether `dissolvedsolids.co` is being linked to by Tatler, The Edge, Eat Drink KL, KL Foodie, MONO Malaysia, Time Out KL, lifestyle blogs, and Malaysian travel guides. Schema and content depth show diminishing returns once the link graph is weak.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP) signals.** Both bars need separate GBP entries with consistent NAP (name, address, phone), regular post cadence, photo uploads (especially recent), review velocity and response rate. Local-pack ranking in "cocktail bar near me" is dominated by GBP, not by your site.
- **Search Console actual queries and CTR.** You should be checking which queries you appear for, which queries you rank for but get no clicks (title/desc problem, tied to points 4-5 above), and which queries you target but never appear for. None of this is visible from on-page audit.
- **Page Experience signals** (Core Web Vitals: LCP, CLS, INP). Cannot measure from static HTML; needs PageSpeed Insights or field data from Chrome UX Report.
- **Local citations.** NAP consistency across Yelp, Foursquare, Time Out KL, Burpple, KL Foodie, Tripadvisor. These feed the local-pack and the Maps panel.
- **Brand search volume.** "Dissolved Solids" or "Soluble Solids" branded queries trending up over time signals to Google that you matter. Tracked in Search Console plus Google Trends.
- **Reviews and review velocity.** Google ranks bars partly by review count plus recency plus response rate. Cannot measure from filesystem.

To verify whether the site "ranks well in all types of queries," the next step is:
1. Connect Search Console and pull a Performance report for the last 28 days, filtered by Malaysia.
2. Export the queries-and-pages CSV and bucket by intent type (geographic, occasion, festivity, recipe).
3. Cross-reference against this audit: any query where you have a page but average position is over 20 indicates either a title/description CTR problem (Part 1 points 2-3) or off-page link gap.
4. Audit GBP for both bars. The cocktail-bar local pack is dominated by GBP-level optimisations, not on-page.

**Bottom line:** The on-page foundation is unusually strong. The biggest at-the-margin lifts are the stale sitemap, the ZH FAQ gap, the three homepage hreflang fixes, and the title/description trimming. Beyond that, actual rank movement will come from off-page signals that need Search Console, GBP, and link-building work outside this codebase.
