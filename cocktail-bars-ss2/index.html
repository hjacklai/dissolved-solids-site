Cocktail bar in SS2.

Soluble Solids is a cocktail parlour in SS2, Petaling Jaya. No printed list. Every drink built bespoke from a short conversation about strength, base spirit, and flavour profile.

Updated May 2026·SS2, Petaling Jaya

SS2 is one of the older commercial blocks in Petaling Jaya. Famous for its night-market food, its dim sum shops, and a tight cluster of small, owner-operated F&B rooms that have quietly turned the area into one of the better drink districts in the Klang Valley. Soluble Solids sits on Jalan SS2/24, on the second floor of a heritage shoplot, around the corner from the SS2 wet market.

Where exactly

Soluble Solids · 50-1 Jalan SS2/24, 47300 Petaling Jaya, Selangor. Second floor. The street is one-way; if you are coming from SS2 main square (where the night market sets up), you will see us on your left as the road bends.

What makes the SS2 location worth the trip

Three things, beyond the drinks:

1. The neighbourhood pace. SS2 does not feel like a clubbing district. The streets are quiet by 22:00, the restaurants close their kitchens by 23:00, and the few late-night spots (us included) are walking distance from each other. A drinking evening here is slow, conversation-led, and unhurried.

2. The food, before or after. Most of our guests eat in SS2 before walking over: the dim sum at the corner shop, the bak kut teh on Jalan SS2/64, the dessert spots, the night market. Tell us where you ate; we will build a drink that pairs.

3. The room itself. 28 seats. Lace curtains in the window. Vintage glassware. The infusion shelf behind the bar is the operational heart, every customised drink starts there.

How customising actually works

The bartender will ask three quick questions:

  • Strength: light (spritz-territory), medium (classic), strong (spirit-forward).
  • Base spirit: gin, rum, whisky, vodka, tequila, mezcal, brandy, or "surprise me".
  • Profile: citrusy, sweet, bitter, herbal, smoky, floral, spicy, tropical, creamy, nutty.

From there, the build comes off the infusion shelf, house-infused spirits, syrups, tinctures, bitters. Each drink takes 3 to 5 minutes. Most guests order 2 to 3 over an evening.

Want to design the drink before you arrive? Use our drink builder on the website, then WhatsApp the result to the bar. We will have it ready as you sit down.

Parking

Street parking on Jalan SS2/24, free after 18:00 and on Sundays. A small public parking lot one block away (Jalan SS2/26) usually has space even at 21:00. Friday and Saturday after 20:00 are tight, plan for a 5-minute walk from your spot.
