Graduation drinks in KL.

A small-bar plan for the post-graduation cocktail celebration in Kuala Lumpur. The mixed-generation family format, drinks that work across the table, NA programme, and a quieter Petaling Jaya alternative.

Graduation gatherings have a specific shape: the graduate is the centre, the table is multi-generational. The drinks have to serve a 22-year-old and a 65-year-old in the same round. A craft cocktail bar in Kuala Lumpur handles this well if you brief in advance. KL also has the rooftop and hotel-bar options if the family wants the city-view treatment; we cover the small-bar version here.

The KL graduation landscape

  • Local universities mostly do their convocation cycles in May-June.
  • International returners (US, UK, Australia) graduate May-July and December.
  • Hotel bars handle the larger 15-25 person family-and-extended-friends gathering.
  • Small craft bars (30-50 seats) handle the 4-12 person intimate gathering best.

What to order at a graduation

French 75: the classic graduation cocktail.

Champagne or prosecco by the glass.

Negroni Sbagliato: Campari, sweet vermouth, prosecco.

Old Fashioned: for the parents and grandparents.

Bellini: peach and prosecco.

The custom graduate drink: ask the bar to build a one-off for the graduate, briefed in advance.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

For a quieter graduation than a KL hotel, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Groups of 6-12 fit comfortably.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, best for 4-8 person graduations. The bespoke build works well for the custom drink.

For non-drinking family members

  • NA French 75.
  • NA Bellini.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • Elderflower spritz.
  • NA Negroni.
  • Sirap selasih: often appreciated by older guests.

The graduation evening plan

Ceremony, photos: daytime.

Dinner, 7pm-9pm: family meal.

Bar, 9pm-11pm: two or three rounds. Custom graduate drink on round one.

After 11pm: graduate may continue with friends; family heads home.

Brief the bar in advance

WhatsApp 5-7 days ahead with the graduate's name, group size, ages, favourite spirits, allergies, non-drinkers, and whether you want a custom drink. We will reply within an hour.
