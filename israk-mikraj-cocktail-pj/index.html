Israk Mikraj in PJ.

A respectful non-alcoholic cocktail-bar plan for Israk Mikraj in Petaling Jaya. NA programme, coffee and tea, quiet considered presentation.

Israk Mikraj (also Isra and Mi'raj) marks the Prophet Muhammad's night journey from Makkah to Jerusalem and ascension through the heavens. A reflective Islamic observance. At a craft cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya, the day calls for the NA programme: serious mocktails, brewed teas, contemplative service.

The NA-focused approach

On Israk Mikraj, our NA programme is the default for observing guests. Same craft, same glassware. The alcoholic side remains available for guests who are not observing.

What to order on Israk Mikraj

The drinks lean reflective and warming.

Saffron and Honey Lemonade: saffron-infused syrup, honey, lemon, soda.

Cardamom and Date Cooler: brewed cardamom tea, date syrup, lemon, ice.

Rose and Pomegranate Sherbet: rose syrup, fresh pomegranate, lemon, soda.

NA Bandung: rose syrup, evaporated milk, ice.

Pandan and Coconut Refresher: pandan syrup, coconut water, lime, soda. Malaysian-local.

Sirap Selasih: rose syrup with basil seeds.

Karak Chai: proper cardamom-led milk tea.

Yemeni Coffee: single-origin Arabica with cardamom.

Our two PJ outlets

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; music softer on Israk Mikraj.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, NA programme as default on the night.
