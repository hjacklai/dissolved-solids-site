Chap Goh Mei in KL.

The 15th night of Chinese New Year, in Kuala Lumpur, at a cocktail bar. Mandarin orange cocktails, the date-night reading of the festival, and a Petaling Jaya alternative when the KL spots get crowded.

Chap Goh Mei (元宵节) closes Chinese New Year on the 15th lunar day. In the Hokkien diaspora it doubles as Chinese Valentine's: unmarried women historically tossed mandarin oranges into the river inscribed with phone numbers and wishes. The cocktail night that fits the festival, then, is light, citrus-forward, slightly playful, and very much a date night. KL has the venues. Here is the plan.

The Chap Goh Mei drink, in three notes

Mandarin orange is the festival fruit. Hawthorn berry is the season's other anchor. Osmanthus, jasmine, and tea-based syrups all read correctly. A cocktail bar that does Chap Goh Mei well puts mandarin or hawthorn in the spotlight without making the drink red and orange just for the photo.

What to order on Chap Goh Mei in KL

Mandarin Negroni: gin, sweet vermouth, Campari, fresh mandarin juice and zest. The festival's signature drink, the one we look forward to all year.

Mandarin and Hawthorn Highball: bourbon, hawthorn syrup, mandarin juice, soda. Long, refreshing, easy to share.

Hawthorn Spritz: hawthorn berry syrup, lemon, prosecco. Tart, refreshing, photographs beautifully under red lantern light.

Osmanthus Sour: whisky, osmanthus syrup, lemon, egg white. Floral, foamy, the right glass for a date.

Jasmine Tea Martini: gin or vodka, lightly brewed jasmine tea, lemon. Clean, light, easy to pair with food.

Pomelo Gimlet: gin, fresh pomelo juice, lime cordial. Pomelo is CNY's other citrus.

Chap Goh Mei as a date night

The festival's romantic reading is what makes it different from the rest of CNY. The actual evening looks like:

Dinner first, 7pm-8:30pm: Chinese restaurant if it is open (many close for the public holiday early in CNY but reopen by Chap Goh Mei). The 15th is often when the holiday's eating is finally over and a real meal returns.

Cocktail at 9pm: the bar with the mandarin or hawthorn programme. If the bartender does not know what Chap Goh Mei is, you are at the wrong bar.

Walk somewhere with red lanterns, 10:30pm-11:30pm: KL central, Petaling Street, Pudu. The festival's last visible decoration night.

Second drink, 11:30pm onward: stirred and quiet. Osmanthus Old Fashioned, a single-malt Scotch with a mandarin twist.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

If KL central is crowded with the last of the CNY tourists, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Mandarin and hawthorn feature for the night.

Soluble Solids · SS2: no printed menu. Tell the bartender it is Chap Goh Mei and that the partner across from you is the entire point of the evening; they will build accordingly.

For non-drinking dates

The CNY observance for many Buddhist and folk-Buddhist families includes vegetarianism on Chap Goh Mei. We pour the same Chap Goh Mei spirit into NA cocktails:

  • NA mandarin highball: fresh mandarin, hawthorn syrup, soda.
  • Hawthorn berry tea cocktail: hawthorn tea, lemon, honey, ice.
  • Osmanthus tea spritz: osmanthus tea, lemon, soda.
  • Pomelo and honey cooler: pomelo juice, honey, lime, soda.
  • Jasmine and rose cooler: jasmine tea, rose syrup, lime, soda.

Reservations

Chap Goh Mei is a romantic-coded but not blockbuster night in KL. The serious cocktail bars are 60-80 percent booked. Two days ahead is enough lead time. WhatsApp ahead for groups of 3 or more.
