Cocktail bar near TTDI.

Eight minutes from Taman Tun Dr Ismail. The closest serious cocktail bar to the TTDI residential area is Dissolved Solids, in Damansara Kim. Tatler Asia Top 20 Bars 2025/26.

Updated May 2026·Damansara Kim, PJ

TTDI is great for almost everything (the market, the kopitiams, Plaza Damas, the parks, the schools). One thing it does not have inside its own neighbourhood is a serious cocktail bar. The good news: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim is an eight-minute drive away, on the other side of the LDP. Most of our regulars from TTDI walk in and out within the same evening without thinking about distance.

The drive from TTDI

From Jalan Datuk Sulaiman (TTDI's main commercial strip): 8 minutes off-peak, 12 minutes during Friday-evening traffic. Cross the LDP, exit at Damansara Utama, then it is two left turns into SS20.

From the TTDI MRT station: 10 minutes by Grab. RM 8-12 fare.

From the TTDI residential streets (Jalan Athinahapan, Jalan Burhanuddin Helmi): 10-15 minutes.

Where exactly

Dissolved Solids · 43-1 Jalan SS20/11, Damansara Kim, 47400 Petaling Jaya. Second floor. Pink neon "dissolved solids" sign in the window. Open in Google Maps →

Why TTDI residents come here

Three reasons we hear most often:

1. It is close. Closer than KL city centre, closer than Mont Kiara, much closer than Bangsar. The 20-minute Grab fare to KLCC for a cocktail evening starts looking unreasonable when there is a Tatler Top 20 bar 8 minutes away.

2. The room is small and quiet. TTDI is a residential area; most of our TTDI guests are looking for a place to sit and talk, not a club. The room seats 32. The music is low enough to hear the person across the table.

3. Customisable everything. The bartender will adjust any drink on the menu, sweetness, strength, base spirit, without making it weird. For TTDI residents who drink regularly and have specific preferences, this matters.

What to expect

The bar opens at 14:00 or 15:00 depending on the day. The first two hours are quiet, ideal for a slow afternoon drink. After 19:00 on Friday and Saturday, the room fills up; book ahead.

Cocktails RM 38-55. Snacks RM 18-32. No corkage, no outside drinks.

The signature picks, if you have not tried us before: Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon), Pandan Collins (gin, pandan, lime, soda), Jungle Bird (1978 KL Hilton classic, done properly).
