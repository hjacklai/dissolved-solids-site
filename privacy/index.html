Privacy, briefly.

A short, honest description of what dissolvedsolids.co collects, what it doesn't, and what we do with anything that crosses our path.

Last updated 16 May 2026

What this site collects

Nothing automatically. dissolvedsolids.co is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't run cookies, trackers, or analytics scripts. We don't fingerprint your browser. We don't know who you are when you arrive, and we don't try to figure it out.

Your browser does send standard HTTP request headers (IP address, user agent, referrer) to GitHub's servers when you load a page. That's how the web works. GitHub may keep these in their server logs under their privacy policy. We don't access or use those logs.

The drink builder

The drink builder runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, the generated recipe, and the share URL are computed on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers because there are no servers, just static files.

The share URL (the part after the # in the address bar) is not transmitted to GitHub or to us. It stays on your device unless you copy and share it.

Reservations and WhatsApp

The reservation form generates a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp on your device. You then choose to send it (or not). The form does not transmit data to our servers; the WhatsApp message goes from your phone to ours, through WhatsApp's own infrastructure.

Once a WhatsApp message reaches us, we treat it as a normal customer enquiry. We keep the conversation thread in our WhatsApp Business account so we can recognise returning guests and refer back to a booking. We don't share these messages with anyone outside the bar staff. We don't sell or pass on phone numbers.

If you'd like us to delete the WhatsApp conversation, message us and we will. WhatsApp also lets you delete messages from your own side at any time.

External requests

Some pages load resources from external services:

  • Google Fonts serves the DM Serif Display and JetBrains Mono fonts used across the site. Google may log requests under their own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps embeds appear on the bar pages with location previews. These embeds are loaded from Google and follow Google's privacy policy.

If you don't want these external requests, browser extensions like uBlock Origin or NoScript can block them without breaking the rest of the site (the fonts will fall back to system serif/mono, the maps will turn into a "open in Google Maps" link).

Children

This site is intended for people of legal drinking age in Malaysia (18+). We don't knowingly collect any data from children. Children are welcome at the bars before 8pm with a parent (see FAQ).

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll update this page and bump the date at the top. We don't send notifications because we don't have your email address.

Questions

WhatsApp Dissolved Solids or Soluble Solids. We answer within the hour during open hours.
