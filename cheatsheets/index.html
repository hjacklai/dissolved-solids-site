How to use these cheat sheets

Open the cheat sheet you want. Use your browser's print menu (Ctrl+P on Windows, Cmd+P on Mac) and either print to paper or save to PDF. Each sheet is designed to fit on a single A4 or US Letter page when printed.

For home use: pin them to the wall behind your bar. For the working bar: laminate them and keep them at the prep station for reference.

Free to use, share, print. If you find them useful, a backlink or social mention is appreciated but not required.
