Easter weekend in PJ.

The Easter weekend cocktail-bar plan in Petaling Jaya. Spring-coded drinks, Sunday-roast-and-cocktail pairings, family-friendly format, NA programme for non-drinking guests.

Easter in Malaysia is a Good-Friday-to-Easter-Monday long weekend (Good Friday is a public holiday in Sabah and Sarawak; the rest of the country gets Easter as a working weekend). For Christian households across the peninsula, the weekend includes Maundy Thursday, Good Friday observance, a quiet Saturday, and the Easter Sunday family gathering. At a craft cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya, the weekend rewards a brunch-leaning Sunday cocktail or a quiet Saturday digestif.

The Easter weekend at a bar

Good Friday: reflective. NA programme more prominent than usual. Coffee, brewed tea, NA cocktails for the observing visit.

Easter Saturday: a normal-ish bar night. Friends gathering, dinner-then-drinks, the usual weekend format.

Easter Sunday: family-and-friends brunch or post-roast cocktails. The big-table version of the weekend.

Easter Monday: wind-down. A quiet drink or two before the work week.

What to order on Easter weekend

The drinks lean spring-coded: floral, citrus-forward, gently sweet, occasionally Easter-egg-coded (chocolate, hazelnut, marzipan).

French 75: gin, lemon, sugar, champagne. The Easter Sunday brunch classic.

Bellini: peach puree, prosecco. Floral, soft.

Aviation: gin, lemon, maraschino, creme de violette. Pale lavender, spring-coded.

Hugo Spritz: elderflower, prosecco, mint, soda. Light long drink.

Chocolate Espresso Martini: vodka, kahlua, espresso, dark chocolate shavings. The Easter egg, in a coupe.

Hazelnut Old Fashioned: bourbon, hazelnut syrup, aromatic bitters. The Easter marzipan side.

Cucumber Gimlet: gin, fresh cucumber, lime, sugar. Spring-clean.

Mimosa: orange juice and prosecco. The Easter brunch default.

Sunday brunch and roast pairings

Easter brunch: Mimosa, Bellini, French 75. Long, light, paired with eggs, ham, hot cross buns.

Easter roast: the protein dictates. Lamb wants a Negroni or a glass of red. Ham wants a Hugo Spritz or a Whisky Sour. Salmon wants champagne or an Aviation.

Easter dessert: Chocolate Espresso Martini, Hazelnut Old Fashioned, or a small glass of vintage port.

For non-drinking guests

Easter family gatherings often include non-drinking guests (children, elderly, non-drinkers by preference). The NA programme:

  • NA Mimosa: orange juice and NA sparkling.
  • Elderflower spritz NA: elderflower cordial, lemon, soda.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • NA Bellini: peach puree and NA sparkling.
  • Hot cross bun-inspired NA hot chocolate on request.
