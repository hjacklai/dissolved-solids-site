Long-form features.

In-depth reviews from Malaysian publications

  • Options, The Edge Malaysia15 August 2022

    Find bold, flavour-forward cocktails at Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim

    At Dissolved Solids, alcoholic beverages are only part of an extensive menu of "all sorts in liquid form" . . . Teetotallers are not left out, as there is a meaty menu of non-alcoholic beverages that have just as much care and thought behind their flavours as the cocktails.

    By Lakshmi Sekhar · 1,000-word review · Mentions Feng Shui, Yellow Oval Crisp, Junos Sour, the "soup of the day" off-menu format

  • Eat Drink KL4 March 2023

    Soluble Solids, SS2 Petaling Jaya

    The bar infuses tequila, gin, whiskies and other spirits with 'soluble solids' that span pistachios and ground nuts to apricot, star anise and cloves . . . Florum (osmanthus, chrysanthemum and jasmine) to Evergreen (torch ginger and tamarind).

    Features the Hornbill house spirit line, Monsoon (tequila + mangosteen + duku langsat), and Neapolitan savoury martini. Also mirrored on the long-running Eat Drink KL Blogspot archive.

  • Eat Drink KLDamansara Kim feature

    Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim

    Snapshot review of the original bar, plus a 2019 Eat Drink KL opening write-up.

  • Tatler Asia20 March 2020

    Dissolved Solids Delivers Ready-To-Drink Cocktails Straight To Your Doorstep

    The pair launched a delivery service offering handcrafted beverages ranging from cappuccinos to cocktails. Their innovation involved separating cocktails from ice and packaging garnishes separately to preserve quality during transit.

    By Samantha Sowerby · MCO-era pivot to RTD cocktails in collectible bottles
