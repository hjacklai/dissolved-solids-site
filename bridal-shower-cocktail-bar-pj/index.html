Bridal shower venue in PJ.

An intimate cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya for a bridal shower, hen party, or bachelorette. Custom drinks named for the bride, real non-alcoholic options for every guest, daytime or evening, 12 to 40 people.

From 12 to 40 guests·Petaling Jaya

A bridal shower wants a room that feels personal, not a function hall with a banner taped to the wall. Our two small cocktail bars in PJ hold 12 to 40 guests, which happens to be the natural bridal-shower size, and the bartender builds the afternoon or evening around the bride rather than a generic package. The drinks stay at craft standard, every guest who is not drinking gets a non-alcoholic version of the same cocktail, and the bill runs cleaner than the equivalent hotel or central KL spend. The trade-off is no ballroom and no view, which is exactly the point: a shower is meant to feel like a room full of the bride's people, not an event held in a venue.

Bridal shower, hen party, bachelorette, kitchen tea

They are different names for variations on the same evening, and we host all of them:

  • Bridal shower. Usually daytime or early evening, mixed ages, gentler energy, often with the bride's family present. Tea-and-cocktails format works well.
  • Hen party or hen night. Evening, friends-led, higher energy. Soluble Solids suits this best; the build-a-drink format becomes a small entertainment.
  • Bachelorette. The same as a hen night by another name. Custom drinks named for the bride, a signature for the guest of honour.
  • Kitchen tea. The lower-key, daytime version. Dissolved Solids opens from the afternoon and suits this.

Daytime shower or evening hen

For a daytime shower or kitchen tea, Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim is the answer. It opens from 14:00 to 15:00, so an afternoon booking is straightforward. Afternoon light, lower volume, a long table for gifts and cake, lighter drinks (spritzes, highballs, low-ABV builds) with strong non-alcoholic options for guests who are driving or not drinking.

For an evening hen night, either outlet works. Soluble Solids in SS2 opens at 18:00 and leans into the evening format; the bespoke-build model means each guest can get a drink made for them, which becomes part of the night rather than just a round of drinks.

The custom drinks

This is the part that makes a cocktail-bar shower different from a restaurant booking. We build a short menu, usually four to six drinks, named for the bride and the occasion, printed on small cards. Typically:

  • A signature cocktail for the bride, the centrepiece of the menu, often built from her favourite flavours.
  • A couple of lighter, sessionable drinks for an afternoon or a long evening.
  • A non-alcoholic version of every cocktail, included by default, so guests who are pregnant, driving, or simply not drinking are not handed a glass of juice as an afterthought.
  • Optional: a small drink named for each guest at Soluble Solids, built on the spot from a one-line brief.

The menu costs nothing extra; it needs 2 to 3 weeks of lead time to build and trial.

Decorations, cake, and games

You bring the candles, banners, balloons, sashes, and props; we set them up before guests arrive. We cut, plate, and serve any cake you bring at no extra charge. Shower games run well in the room; tell us the plan and we pace the bar around it so the drinks land between rounds rather than on top of them. Outside food, whether a Grab order or proper catering, is welcome, and we can coordinate catering through a partner kitchen for an additional charge.

What it costs

We use a minimum spend rather than a flat venue fee. Guest drinks count toward it, so a well-attended shower usually meets the minimum naturally and the room effectively costs nothing on top of what guests would have spent anyway.

  • Weekday afternoon or evening, 12 to 30 guests: RM 3,000 to RM 6,000 minimum spend.
  • Friday or Saturday, 25 to 40 guests: RM 6,500 to RM 9,500 minimum spend.
  • Partial-section booking for 12 to 20 guests: smaller minimum, ask for a quote.

PJ rates run roughly 20 to 30% below an equivalent central KL or hotel shower for the same group.

How to book

WhatsApp the outlet 3 to 6 weeks ahead with:

  • The date, and whether you want daytime or evening.
  • Guest count and likely range.
  • The bride's favourite flavours, and anything to avoid (allergies, a flavour she dislikes).
  • Whether you want a custom menu and named drinks.
  • Decoration, cake, and game plans.
  • Any guests who are pregnant or not drinking, so we plan the non-alcoholic side properly.

We reply within 24 hours with a proposed format, the minimum spend, and confirmation of availability.
