Cocktail bar near Puchong.

Puchong's cocktail-bar scene is light. The closest serious cocktail bars are 25 to 28 minutes north in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Puchong is a large suburban township with a strong dining scene (the bak kut teh, the seafood, the dim sum) and a thin cocktail-bar scene. If you live in Puchong, Kinrara, IOI Mall area, or Bandar Puchong Jaya and want a serious cocktail evening, the 25-minute drive north into central PJ is the most reliable option.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11, 47400 PJ. Open Tue-Sun. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24, 47300 PJ. Open Wed-Sun, 18:00 onwards. Maps · WhatsApp

The drive from Puchong

  • From IOI Mall Puchong: 25 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 28 minutes to Soluble Solids.
  • From Bandar Puchong Jaya: 23 minutes / 26 minutes.
  • From Kinrara: 22 minutes / 25 minutes.
  • From Setia Walk: 25 minutes / 28 minutes.
  • From Bandar Puteri Puchong: 27 minutes / 30 minutes.

Route: take the LDP heading north all the way. Exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, continue north and exit at Section 17 for Soluble Solids. Mostly highway, predictable.

Grab fares from Puchong: RM 28-40 to either bar.

Why drive 25 minutes

Honestly, because the alternative is a much longer drive into KL city centre for a comparable cocktail experience. From most of Puchong, our PJ outlets are nearly as quick as the KLCC corridor and the parking is much easier.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Thu 15:00-00:00, Fri-Sat 14:00-01:00, Sun 14:00-00:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun 18:00-01:00.

Walk-ins welcome. WhatsApp ahead for groups of 6 or more or for Saturday after 21:00.
