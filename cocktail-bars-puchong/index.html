Cocktail bar near Puchong.

Puchong's cocktail-bar scene is light. The closest serious cocktail bars are 25 to 28 minutes north in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Puchong is one of the largest suburban townships in Selangor, anchored by IOI Mall and stretching south through Bandar Kinrara, Setia Walk, and Bandar Puteri all the way to the Cyberjaya edge. Strong dining scene (bak kut teh, Cantonese seafood, dim sum, mamak corners), but a thin specialty cocktail-bar scene. For Puchong residents who want a serious cocktail evening, the 22 to 28 minute drive north into central PJ is the most reliable answer.

The character of Puchong

Puchong runs along the LDP corridor on a north-south axis, divided into named sub-townships. Bandar Puchong Jaya and IOI Mall area are the older commercial-residential core, with the densest food scene and the bulk of the F and B. Bandar Kinrara and Taman Kinrara are mid-tier residential blocks to the east. Bandar Puteri Puchong and Bandar Puchong Utama are the newer southern developments. Setia Walk is the outdoor-mall-and-condo cluster between the two.

Food is genuinely deep in Puchong. The bak kut teh row near IOI Mall, the Cantonese seafood places along Jalan Wawasan, the dim sum at Tasty Pot, the late-night mamaks all around Bandar Puchong Jaya. The drinking scene is mostly chain bars, gastropubs, and karaoke lounges; the small specialist cocktail-bar format (50-seat room, 60-bottle back bar, fresh-cut citrus, customised builds) is rare inside Puchong itself.

Why drive north for the drink

Puchong's commercial real estate is mostly mall-anchored or shoplot-anchored; both formats favour higher-volume venues over small specialist rooms. A serious cocktail bar trades volume for craft and needs the surrounding dinner density of PJ's older corridors (SS2, Damansara Kim, Section 17). The LDP gives Puchong a fast direct route north, which makes the trade worth it.

For most of Puchong, our PJ outlets are nearly as quick as driving to the KLCC corridor, and the parking situation is much easier (free street parking, no RM 15 to 20 mall fee, no hunting for a slot). That tilts the maths toward PJ for the Puchong-resident evening.

The route is straightforward: take the LDP northbound all the way. Exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, or continue one more interchange north to Section 17 for Soluble Solids. The LDP is mostly highway-grade and predictable; off-peak the drive is a single uninterrupted run.

Friday evening 17:30 to 19:00 the LDP northbound through Sunway and Subang Jaya is the slow stretch; budget 35 minutes from southern Puchong. After 19:30 the traffic clears and the drive is back to 22 to 28 minutes. Saturday afternoon is the cleanest window.

The Sri Petaling/Putra Heights LRT line runs through Puchong; from Pusat Bandar Puchong, IOI Puchong Jaya, or Taman Perindustrian Puchong stations, ride to Universiti, change to the Kelana Jaya line, exit at Taman Bahagia, then 8-minute Grab to either bar. About 55 minutes total, useful if the driver wants to drink.

Grab fares from Puchong: RM 28 to 42 to either bar.

What to order if it is your first time

At Dissolved Solids, two house signatures to start with: the Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon, foamed) and the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan, lime, soda). If you have just come from a heavy Cantonese seafood dinner in Puchong, the Negroni here cuts through richness well, served stirred and cold with a single large clear cube.

At Soluble Solids, skip the printed list. Tell the bartender three short answers: strength (light, medium, strong), spirit (gin, rum, whisky, mezcal, surprise), flavour direction (citrusy, herbal, smoky, sweet). The drink is built in front of you from the wall of labelled infusion jars.

Hours and when to come

Dissolved Solids: Tuesday to Thursday 15:00 to 00:00, Friday and Saturday 14:00 to 01:00, Sunday 14:00 to 00:00. Closed Mondays.

Soluble Solids: Wednesday to Sunday 18:00 to 01:00. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

Walk-ins are welcome at both. WhatsApp ahead for groups of six or more or for Friday/Saturday after 21:00. For a quick post-work drink, Dissolved Solids opens earliest. For a longer settled evening, Soluble Solids reads better.

Getting back to Puchong after

The drive back via the LDP is fast post-midnight, 18 to 24 minutes with no traffic. Police checkpoints occasionally operate on the LDP Friday and Saturday nights; never drive after a drink. Grab is reliable for the return at 01:00 but typically surge-priced on weekends (budget RM 35 to 55 back).
