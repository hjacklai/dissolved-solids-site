Israk Mikraj in KL.

A respectful non-alcoholic cocktail-bar plan for Israk Mikraj in Kuala Lumpur. NA programme, coffee and tea, quieter Petaling Jaya alternative.

Israk Mikraj marks the Prophet's night journey and ascension. A reflective Islamic observance. At a craft cocktail bar in Kuala Lumpur, the day calls for the NA programme: serious mocktails, brewed teas, single-origin coffee, served with the same care as the regular menu.

What to order in KL on Israk Mikraj

Saffron and Honey Lemonade: saffron syrup, honey, lemon, soda.

Cardamom and Date Cooler: cardamom tea, date syrup, lemon, ice.

Rose and Pomegranate Sherbet: rose syrup, pomegranate, lemon, soda.

NA Bandung: rose syrup, evaporated milk, ice.

Pandan and Coconut Refresher: pandan syrup, coconut water, lime, soda.

Karak Chai: cardamom-led milk tea, iced or hot.

Yemeni Coffee: Arabica with cardamom.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

For a quieter, more contemplative room, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, NA programme as default on the night.

For mixed-faith groups

Both sides poured in parallel. Matching glassware.

Reservations

Walk-ins generally work. For groups of 4 or more, WhatsApp ahead.
