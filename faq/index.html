Reservations and walk-ins

Do I need to reserve a table?
Walk-ins are welcome at both bars. Weekends fill up after 8pm. Weeknights and earlier evenings, we usually have space. If you want certainty, reserve a table.

How do I make a reservation?
Through our reservation form, which sends a WhatsApp message to the bar of your choice. We confirm within an hour during open hours.

Is there a deposit or cancellation fee?
No deposit. No cancellation fee. We do appreciate a heads-up if you can't make it.

How far in advance should I book?
A day ahead is plenty for weeknights. Two to three days ahead for weekends. Same-day works most of the time, though we can't always promise the seat you want.

Hours and getting there

What are your opening hours?

  • Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: Tue, Wed, Thu 15:00 to 00:00. Fri, Sat 14:00 to 01:00. Sun 14:00 to 00:00. Mon closed.
  • Soluble Solids · SS2: Wed to Sun 18:00 to 01:00. Mon, Tue closed.

Where exactly are you?

  • Dissolved Solids: 43-1 Jalan SS20/11, Damansara Kim, 47400 Petaling Jaya.
  • Soluble Solids: 50-1 Jalan SS2/24, 47300 Petaling Jaya.

Do you have parking?
Both bars have street parking nearby. Damansara Kim is slightly easier than SS2, on average. Parking fills up after 8pm on weekends. We recommend arriving by 7:30pm if you're driving.

Public transport?
MRT Kajang Line stops at Bandar Utama (about 10 minutes by Grab to either bar). KTM Komuter has Petaling station, also about 10 minutes by Grab.

Drinks and dietary

Do you have non-alcoholic options?
Yes, real ones. Our coffee mocktails (Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom) are built with the same care as the cocktails. We also pour our own kombuchas when ready, plus loose-leaf teas and proper coffee. More on our mocktail philosophy here.

Can I customise a drink?
Yes. At Soluble Solids, customising is the default since there's no fixed list. At Dissolved Solids, the menu is short but the bartender will adjust most drinks if you ask. You can also use the drink builder to design something in five taps before you arrive.

Vegan? Gluten-free? Allergies?
Most of our cocktails can be made vegan (aquafaba in place of egg white). Most can be made gluten-free. Please tell the bartender about allergies before ordering.

What snacks do you have?
A small kitchen menu. See /snacks/ for the current list.

Pricing

How much do drinks cost?
Cocktails are between RM 35 and RM 60 depending on the spirit and the build. Mocktails between RM 25 and RM 35. Coffee and tea between RM 12 and RM 22. We don't list prices on the website because they shift slightly with bottle costs, but we keep them within Damansara Kim and SS2 ranges, not KLCC ranges.

Service charge?
6 percent SST is included on the bill. No service charge.

Payment?
Cash, cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), and Malaysian e-wallets (GrabPay, Touch 'n Go). No surcharge on cards.

Group, kids, pets

Group size?
Solo, twos, threes, and small groups (up to about six) are the comfort zone for both rooms. Larger groups are possible but please WhatsApp ahead.

Can I bring children?
Welcome before 8pm at both bars, with a parent. After 8pm, the rooms get smokier and the music goes up.

Pets?
Service animals welcome. Other pets, please WhatsApp ahead.

Wheelchair accessibility?
Dissolved Solids has a small step at the entrance and a tight interior. Soluble Solids is mostly step-free at the entrance but has tight indoor spots. WhatsApp ahead and we'll prepare the easiest seating.

Other

Dress code?
None. KL casual is the default.

Smoking?
Indoor smoking is not allowed under Malaysian law. Both bars have a small outdoor area suitable for smoking.

Corkage?
We don't offer corkage as standard. For a special bottle on an occasion, WhatsApp ahead and we'll see what we can arrange.

Outside food?
Generally no, since we have a snacks menu. Birthday cakes are an exception and welcome with notice.

Private events / buyouts?
Possible at both bars. WhatsApp Dissolved Solids or Soluble Solids for a quote.

Question not answered?

WhatsApp the bar directly. Replies usually within the hour during open hours.
