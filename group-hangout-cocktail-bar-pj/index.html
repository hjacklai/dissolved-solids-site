Group hangout cocktail bar in PJ.

How to take 4-12 friends out for cocktails in Petaling Jaya. Small bars that handle groups well, what to brief in advance, ordering strategy, and how to make sure the non-drinkers in the group are looked after.

PJ small bars are not built like the larger central-KL group-bar venues. The rooms are smaller, the staff smaller, the bartender often a one-person show. Groups can absolutely work here, but only if the bar knows you are coming. Brief in advance and a 6-12 person hangout at a craft PJ bar is excellent. Walk in cold with 10 friends on a Friday night and it will go poorly.

Why brief us in advance, even for a casual hangout

A craft cocktail bar at 30 seats can absolutely handle 10 of you, but only if we know you are coming. Three practical reasons:

  • Seating layout. If we know it is 10, we will set up the long table or combine bar-counter sections. If we do not, you arrive to a configuration meant for couples.
  • Ingredient prep. If the group is 10 and four of you are going to order Negronis, we want to make sure we have enough sweet vermouth on the night. Without notice, we might run out mid-round-two.
  • Pace expectations. The bartender can pace a group's first round more efficiently if they know the group exists. 10 drinks made at once takes 18-22 minutes; 10 drinks made as walk-ins takes 35.

The PJ group-friendly small-bar setup

For groups of 4-6: most small PJ craft bars can handle this with 24 hours notice.

For groups of 7-12: 5-7 days notice. The bar will set aside a section.

For groups of 13+: a partial or full buyout. See private events.

Our two PJ outlets, for groups

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. We can seat groups of 6-12 by combining the long table with adjacent bar-counter sections. Capacity is about 30 total, so a group of 12 is a third of the room. We brief the rest of the room (regulars who reserve the same night) that a private-ish group will be there.

Soluble Solids · SS2: the smaller of the two. We can host groups up to 8 comfortably; 10 is the absolute max. No printed menu. The bartender will brief on the format before you arrive.

Group ordering strategy

Round one is the slowest. 10 people, 10 different drinks, takes 18-22 minutes. Order at the table, not the bar. The bartender will batch by drink type (all the Negronis together, all the sours together).

Round two is "cluster". By round two, the group has identified 2-3 drinks people are gravitating to. "Three more Old Fashioneds, two more Negronis, two Aperol Spritzes." Faster.

Round three: tell the bartender to surprise the table. They will pour something interesting on the bar's own taste. This is the moment of the night where the bar earns its keep.

Snacks: we have a small bar-snack menu (cheese, charcuterie, olives, nuts, popcorn). Order one or two boards per six people for the long sessions.

For groups with non-drinkers

The NA programme at a serious small bar is real. Same glassware, same care. The non-drinker should never get the lesser version.

Order list for the non-drinkers in your group:

  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey (espresso + palm sugar), Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • NA Negroni: Seedlip + NA Campari + NA vermouth.
  • NA Old Fashioned: NA whisky + demerara + bitters.
  • NA spritzes: elderflower, hibiscus, lemongrass.
  • Kombucha-by-the-glass: when fresh.
  • Brewed tea cocktails: osmanthus, white tea, jasmine.

The PJ group hangout evening plan

Dinner first, 6:30pm-8:30pm: a restaurant nearby. PJ has many group-friendly Chinese, Indian, Western, and Malaysian restaurants. Walk to the bar after.

Bar, 9pm-midnight: three rounds across three hours. Conversation, snacks, the bartender's third-round pick. Most PJ small bars close at midnight or 1am.

Reservations

Groups of 4-6: WhatsApp 2-3 days ahead. Groups of 7-12: 5-7 days ahead. Include the group size, any drink preferences, and any non-drinkers.
