Cocktail bar with parking in PJ.

The unglamorous question that decides where you actually drink. Where to find a cocktail bar in PJ with reliable parking, free after-hours street parking, and covered lots within a 3-minute walk.

5 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

The most important factor when picking a cocktail bar in Petaling Jaya is rarely the drinks list. It is whether you can park. Anyone who drives in KL or PJ on a Friday night knows that "the place we love" loses to "the place where we can park" almost every time. This is the parking-first guide.

Why PJ wins this category over KL

Street parking in most PJ commercial zones (Damansara Kim, SS2, TTDI commercial strips) is free after 18:00 and on Sundays. Covered municipal parking lots in PJ are typically RM 2-3/hour vs RM 5-8/hour in KLCC.

The math: a 4-hour cocktail evening in PJ usually costs RM 0 to 12 for parking. The same evening in KLCC costs RM 20-40 in parking alone, before you have ordered a drink.

This is a structural advantage that quietly shapes the KL-PJ cocktail scene. Many of the better operators have set up shop in PJ partly because the parking helps fill seats.

Our two outlets, the parking situation

Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim). Street parking on Jalan SS20/11 is free after 18:00. Spaces fill from 19:30 on Friday and Saturday; weeknights are easy until 21:00. A covered municipal lot is a 3-minute walk away on Jalan SS20/10, RM 2/hour, almost always has space.

Soluble Solids (SS2). Street parking on Jalan SS2/24 is free after 18:00 and Sundays. A small public parking lot one block away on Jalan SS2/26 usually has space even at 21:00. Friday and Saturday after 20:00 are tight; plan for a 3-5 minute walk from your spot.

Both bars are above shoplots on the second floor, not in a mall, so the parking is street-based rather than mall-based. The trade-off: you have to walk a block sometimes; you do not have to navigate a multi-storey carpark on the way out at 01:00.

The parking-first decision tree

If parking is your highest priority and you are choosing between PJ neighbourhoods:

Easiest parking: Damansara Kim, SS2 weeknights, Section 17 weeknights.

Moderately easy: Damansara Utama (Uptown PJ), TTDI commercial strip, Bandar Sunway.

Hardest in PJ: Bandar Utama (1Utama mall area) on Fri-Sat, Mutiara Damansara during dinner peak, Sunway Pyramid evenings.

Avoid if parking matters: Bangsar on Friday and Saturday nights (legendary nightmare), and most of KL city centre.

Three rules for parking-stress-free drinking

1. Arrive before 19:30 on weekends. Even in easy-parking neighbourhoods, the window after 19:30 turns. Coming earlier is the simplest parking hack.

2. Take Grab if you are uncertain. A Grab fare to PJ from KL is usually RM 18-28 off-peak. If you would spend 20 minutes circling for parking and another 20 minutes walking back, the maths often favour Grab.

3. Designate a driver if you are a group of 3 or more. One person stays mostly sober, drives the round trip, gets a soft drink discount most places (we comp the designated driver a non-alcoholic cocktail at both bars). Spreads the cost and the responsibility.

Public transport context

If you are coming by LRT/MRT:

  • To Damansara Kim: Taman Bahagia LRT (5-7 minute Grab) or Bandar Utama MRT (7-10 minute Grab).
  • To SS2: Taman Bahagia LRT (5-minute Grab) is closest.
  • The bus network reaches both areas but timing is unreliable after 21:00.

Most of our late-evening guests Grab in and Grab out, even those who drove earlier in the day. The parking-and-then-Grab pattern is common.

One more practical note

If you are bringing a group of 6 or more by car: please WhatsApp ahead. We will give you the specific street that always has space at the time you are arriving, and which side of the block to enter from. Local knowledge saves 20 minutes.

The honest summary

Both of our bars are easier to park at than most KL options and equal to or better than most PJ options. Damansara Kim is the easier of the two on Friday and Saturday after 21:00, mostly because SS2 has a more compact commercial grid that fills up faster.

If parking is your top concern: we are open Tuesday to Sunday. The best parking is consistently between 18:00 and 19:30 on weeknights. Walk in.
