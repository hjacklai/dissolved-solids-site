The best time to visit a cocktail bar in PJ.

When to come for the quietest seats, the best bartender attention, the easiest parking, and the longest possible night. A timing guide from the people running two cocktail bars in PJ.

5 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

"What time should we come?" is one of the most common questions we get on WhatsApp. The answer depends entirely on what kind of evening you want. Here is the timing map, broken down by what you are optimising for.

If you want the quietest seats

Best: Tuesday to Thursday, 15:00 to 18:00. Dissolved Solids opens at 15:00 on weekdays. The first 90 minutes are almost empty. The bartender has time to chat. The light through the window is good. Music quiet.

Next best: Sunday, 14:00 to 17:00. Long, slow Sunday afternoons are our favourite shift. Half the room is empty. The drinkers who come are unhurried.

Avoid for quiet: Friday and Saturday after 20:00. Both bars are at peak capacity. You will be seated, but the room is at its loudest and the bartender is moving fast.

If you want the most bartender attention

Same answer as "quietest seats". When the room is empty, the bartender will explain what they are doing, recommend variations, and pour you small tastes of unusual spirits to compare. When the room is busy, the same bartender is making drinks for 30 people simultaneously.

If you are new to cocktails and want to learn, come on a Tuesday at 16:00. You will get a more educational evening than at any "intro to cocktails" class.

If you want the easiest parking

Best: any weekday before 19:30. Street parking is plentiful, free after 18:00, no walking required.

Tight: Friday and Saturday after 20:00. Parking is still findable but expect to walk a block.

See our cocktail bar with parking in PJ article for the full parking guide.

If you want the longest possible night

Best: Friday or Saturday, arrive 19:30, leave 01:00. Both bars close at 01:00 those nights. Arriving at 19:30 gives you 5.5 hours of operating time. The bar's energy peaks between 21:00 and 23:30; the final 90 minutes are softer and more conversational.

Plan B: Wednesday or Thursday, arrive 18:00, leave 00:00. Six hours. Quieter throughout. Better for slow drinkers.

If you want to come straight after work

Best: Tuesday to Friday, 17:30 to 18:30. Dissolved Solids has been open since 15:00; you walk in to a relaxed room. Soluble Solids opens at 18:00; arriving at 18:15 means you are first or second in.

If your office is in Damansara Heights, Bangsar, or Mont Kiara, you can leave at 18:00 and be seated at the bar by 18:20. The 18:00-19:30 hour is consistently the sweet spot for after-work drinks.

If you are celebrating something

Best: Saturday, 19:30 to 22:30. The room has energy without being out of control. Music is at its best level. Friends are easy to gather. WhatsApp ahead so we know to reserve a good corner.

For a birthday or anniversary specifically, see groups and celebrations at a cocktail bar in PJ.

If you want a date night

Best: Wednesday or Thursday, 19:00 to 21:30. Quiet enough to talk. Music at conversation-friendly level. The room has some other couples but not packed. You will not have to lean across the table to hear each other.

Avoid Friday and Saturday after 20:30 for a first date — the noise level makes conversation work harder than it should.

See date night at a cocktail bar in PJ for more.

If you want to taste several drinks

Best: any weekday, arrive 15:00 or 16:00. Pace yourself. Order two or three small-format drinks over two hours, then food, then a final drink. The early-afternoon timing means you are home at a reasonable hour and you actually remember what you tasted.

Ask for a "tasting flight" — three small pours of contrasting spirits or three small variations on a base recipe. We do this off-menu most weekdays.

The honest summary by goal

  • Quietest: Tuesday-Thursday, 15:00-18:00.
  • Most bartender attention: same as above.
  • Easiest parking: any weekday before 19:30.
  • Longest night: Friday or Saturday, arrive 19:30.
  • After work: Tuesday-Friday, 17:30-18:30.
  • Celebration energy: Saturday 19:30-22:30.
  • Date night: Wednesday or Thursday, 19:00-21:30.
  • Tasting flight: any weekday, 15:00-17:00.

Whatever the goal, WhatsApp ahead helps us seat you faster — especially for tighter windows.
