Iced lemon tea, deconstructed.

The kopitiam classic, reimagined behind a bar. What makes a real iced lemon tea, what makes a fake one, and three cocktails that use the format as a base.

4 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

Iced lemon tea is the Malaysian kopitiam staple. Every coffee shop pours it; every menu lists it; every Malaysian has an opinion on which kopitiam makes it best. Behind a serious bar, the iced lemon tea format becomes a surprisingly flexible cocktail base, citrusy, sessionable, low-ABV when you want it, easy to build on.

What a real iced lemon tea is

The Malaysian kopitiam version: strong-brewed black tea (usually a Ceylon or Boh blend), sweetened with sugar syrup, served over ice with fresh lemon wedges. The tea is brewed hot and concentrated, then cooled. Some kopitiams add a slice of preserved sour plum (asam boi) to amplify the sour-sweet contrast.

The character: bright, lightly tannic, sour-sweet, refreshing in 32°C heat. The original Malaysian iced lemon tea is one of the great hot-climate drinks.

What a fake iced lemon tea is

Bottled "iced lemon tea" from a tetra pak or PET bottle. Made from tea powder, citric acid, sugar syrup, and lemon flavouring. The flavour is one-dimensional and overly sweet.

If you are building cocktails on iced lemon tea, brew the base from real tea leaves. Bottled product will not work.

The base recipe (kopitiam-style)

  1. Strong-brew 10g of Boh black tea (or Ceylon) in 400ml of just-boiled water for 5 minutes.
  2. Strain. Add 50g of sugar (or 80ml of palm sugar syrup) while still hot. Stir until dissolved.
  3. Cool to room temperature.
  4. Refrigerate. Lasts 4-5 days.

To serve as a non-cocktail drink: pour over ice with fresh lemon wedges, optionally with a sour plum.

Three cocktails built on iced lemon tea

1. Long Island Iced Tea (the kopitiam version). Vodka + gin + rum + tequila + triple sec, equal parts (15ml each), plus 30ml iced lemon tea base, plus 15ml fresh lemon. Build over ice in a tall glass. The classic Long Island with the kopitiam tea base instead of cola. Higher-quality, less sweet.

2. Whisky Iced Lemon Tea. Whisky (45ml), iced lemon tea base (90ml), fresh lemon juice (15ml), 1 asam boi muddled in. Build over ice in a tall glass. The Malaysian whisky highball. Drinks like a low-ABV punch.

3. Iced Lemon Tea Spritz (low-ABV). Iced lemon tea base (60ml), Aperol or Campari (30ml), top with soda. Build in a wine glass over ice. Garnish with a lemon wheel and a sprig of mint. A Malaysian-Italian fusion that works better than it should.

The asam boi connection

Adding a preserved sour plum (asam boi) to iced lemon tea is a small kopitiam tradition that translates beautifully to cocktails. The salty-sour plum amplifies the tea's tannin and the lemon's acid simultaneously.

In a cocktail format: muddle one asam boi into the bottom of the glass before building. Or add 5ml of asam boi syrup. See our piece on asam boi in cocktails.
