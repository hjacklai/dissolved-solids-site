Decaf coffee cocktails.

Why decaf coffee cocktails are an underrated category, how to brew decaf without ruining the flavour, and the late-night drinks where they actually make sense.

4 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

Decaf coffee cocktails sound like a contradiction. The point of the Espresso Martini, the argument goes, is the caffeine, that's why it shows up at 23:30, after the dinner-coffee window has closed. But there is a real case for decaf in cocktails, and a small handful of guests will ask for it once you make the option clear.

Who orders decaf cocktails

Three groups:

1. Caffeine-sensitive guests. Some people genuinely cannot drink caffeinated coffee after 18:00 without insomnia. They still want the coffee-cocktail flavour profile.

2. Pregnant guests. Caffeine intake during pregnancy is limited (most doctors recommend under 200mg/day). A decaf Espresso Martini-style mocktail is one of the few "treat" options.

3. Recovery / lifestyle reasons. People who have cut caffeine for medical or wellness reasons.

The numbers are small but real. At our bars, roughly 1 in 50 coffee-cocktail orders specifies decaf. Worth keeping the option behind the back shelf.

Why most decaf coffee in cocktails fails

Two reasons:

1. The cheap-decaf problem. Industrial decaffeination strips flavour along with caffeine. Cheap decaf coffee tastes muted, slightly chemical, and one-dimensional. A cocktail built on bad decaf reads as a hollow version of the real thing.

2. The "we don't really care" effect. Many bars treat decaf as an afterthought, brewing it weak, holding it old, serving it past its peak. The drinker tastes the lack of care immediately.

Decaffeination methods, ranked

Swiss Water Process (best): chemical-free, retains most flavour. Premium decaf beans use this method. Recommended for cocktail use.

CO2 supercritical: also chemical-free, very good flavour retention. Slightly cheaper, slightly less common.

Methylene chloride or ethyl acetate (skip): the cheap industrial methods. Flavour is significantly degraded. Avoid for cocktails.

Always check the label or ask the roaster. "Decaffeinated by [process]" should be specified.

Three decaf coffee cocktails worth pouring

1. Decaf Espresso Martini. Vodka (45ml), coffee liqueur (25ml), fresh decaf espresso pulled within 30 seconds (30ml), simple syrup (10ml). Shake hard. Double-strain. Same recipe as the standard, only the espresso changes. With Swiss-Water-decaf, indistinguishable from the caffeinated version.

2. Decaf Cold Brew Old Fashioned. Bourbon (45ml), cold-brewed Swiss-Water-decaf (15ml), demerara syrup (10ml), chocolate bitters (2 dashes). Stir over a large cube. Late-night nightcap that does not keep you up.

3. Decaf Espresso Martini "mocktail" (zero-proof). Skip vodka and coffee liqueur. Use decaf espresso, oat-milk cream, chocolate bitters, and a touch of vanilla syrup. Dry shake then hard shake. For pregnant guests or designated drivers wanting the Espresso-Martini moment.

Sourcing in Malaysia

Swiss-Water decaf beans: any specialty roaster will stock them on request. Common Man, Feeka, VCR in KL all have decaf options. RM 35-55 per 250g, slightly more than caffeinated equivalents.

At a regular cafe or kopitiam, decaf is rarely available. Ask before assuming.
