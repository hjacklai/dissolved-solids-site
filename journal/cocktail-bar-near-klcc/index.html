Cocktail bar near KLCC.

KLCC has plenty of cocktail bars and lounges. A 22-minute drive west into PJ trades rooftop crowds and parking nightmares for quieter rooms and a better drinks-per-ringgit ratio.

5 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

KLCC is the centre of the loud, sky-high lounge format. It is excellent for what it is: large rooms, panoramic city views, bottle service, bachelorette energy. What it is less good for is the small, quiet, drink-focused evening. If that is what you are after, the short drive west into PJ pays off. Here is the case.

Drive times from KLCC

  • To Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim, PJ): 22 minutes off-peak, 30-35 minutes during Friday peak.
  • To Soluble Solids (SS2, PJ): 25 minutes off-peak, 32-38 minutes during Friday peak.

Grab fares from KLCC: RM 20-30 to either bar. Surge multipliers rare except after 22:00 on Friday and Saturday.

What you trade and what you gain

You trade: the view, the rooftop format, the late-club energy that some KLCC venues have, the walking-distance density (in KLCC you can hop between three bars in 10 minutes; in PJ they are spread out).

You gain:

  • Quieter rooms. Most PJ cocktail bars run music well under conversation level. Many KLCC venues do not after 21:00.
  • Easier parking. Free street parking after 18:00 vs RM 5-8/hour at most KLCC venues.
  • Better drinks-per-ringgit. PJ rent is 30-50% lower than KLCC, which lets PJ operators invest more of the cocktail price into spirit selection and bartender training rather than into interior glamour.
  • Bartender attention. Smaller rooms = more time per guest. The bartender will explain what they are pouring, recommend variations.

When the KLCC scene wins

Honestly: when you want the view, when you have out-of-town guests who want the KL skyline experience, when you are part of a larger group celebrating something with bottle service, when you want to walk between several bars in one evening, when you want post-22:00 club energy.

For these use cases, KLCC is the right answer. We are not claiming the PJ outlets are universally better.

When the PJ drive wins

The opposite cases. When you want a quiet date, when you want to focus on the drinks, when parking matters, when you want a smaller room, when you want the bartender to remember your face by visit three.

The route

From KLCC: take Jalan Ampang heading west, join the Federal Highway, exit at Damansara Utama (for Dissolved Solids) or continue and exit at Section 17 (for Soluble Solids). The LDP is an alternative slightly-longer route that avoids the Federal Highway congestion.

Other KL neighbourhoods within similar reach

If you are already considering the drive west, the other KL-side neighbourhoods that suit our bars equally well:

  • Bukit Bintang: 25 minutes.
  • Star Hill / Pavilion: 25 minutes.
  • KL Sentral: 18-22 minutes.
  • Bangsar: 15-18 minutes (closer than KLCC).
  • Mont Kiara: 14-17 minutes (closer than KLCC).

Try it once

The 22-minute drive feels longer in your head than it does on the road. If you usually drink in KLCC and have never tried the PJ corridor, one trip on a Wednesday or Thursday evening is the easiest way to see whether the trade-offs work for you. Many of our regular KL guests now make the trip 2-3 times a month and have stopped wondering about it.

Frequently asked questions

How far is a PJ cocktail bar from KLCC?

Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim is 22 minutes off-peak from KLCC; Soluble Solids in SS2 is 25 minutes. Friday peak adds 10 to 15 minutes. Grab fares run RM 20 to 30 each way. Take Jalan Ampang west, join the Federal Highway, exit at Damansara Utama or Section 17. The LDP is the alternative route that avoids Federal Highway congestion.

What's the difference between KLCC and PJ cocktail bars?

KLCC delivers rooftop views, large rooms, walking-distance density, and post-22:00 club energy. PJ bars trade those for quieter rooms (music well under conversation level after 21:00), free street parking after 18:00, more bartender attention in smaller rooms, and a better drinks-per-ringgit ratio (PJ rent is 30 to 50 percent lower than KLCC, so more of the price goes into spirit selection and bartender training).

When is KLCC the better choice over PJ?

When you want the KL skyline view, when out-of-town guests want the rooftop experience, when you are part of a large group with bottle service, when you want to walk between several bars in one evening, or when you want late club energy after 22:00. For these the city centre is the right answer. PJ wins on the opposite cases.

Can I substitute the KLCC drink budget for a better PJ one?

Effectively yes. A RM 65 cocktail in a KLCC rooftop venue is paying for the rent, the view, and the marketing. A RM 50 to 60 cocktail in PJ at the same quality tier puts more of the budget into the spirit selection and the bartender's time. For drink-focused evenings the PJ economics give you more for less.

Which PJ cocktail bar should I try first from KLCC?

Dissolved Solids (43-1 Jalan SS20/11, Damansara Kim, WhatsApp +60 11-4008 7607) is the easier first visit: 22 minutes off-peak from KLCC, printed cocktail menu, Tatler Asia Top 20 Bars 2025/26 listing. Soluble Solids (50-1 Jalan SS2/24, WhatsApp +60 11-1682 8651) is the deeper visit, a customisation parlour with no printed list. Try Dissolved Solids first, Soluble Solids on visit two.
