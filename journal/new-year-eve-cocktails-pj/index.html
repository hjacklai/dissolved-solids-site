New Year's Eve cocktails in PJ.

How to plan a New Year's Eve cocktail evening in Petaling Jaya. What to order, how to book, what time to arrive, and the small things that make NYE work without becoming chaos.

5 min read·Notes from Dissolved Solids

New Year's Eve is the busiest single night of the year for KL and PJ cocktail bars. The good rooms book out a week ahead; the great rooms run special menus and longer hours. Here is the working plan if you want to drink well rather than queue and crowd.

Book early. Like, really early.

For the established serious cocktail bars in PJ, NYE reservations typically open in late November and fill within a fortnight. By mid-December, you are looking at backup options.

Our reservation pattern: WhatsApp opens November 15 every year for NYE. Both outlets confirmed by December 5. Walk-ins on the night are theoretically possible but realistically very tight.

If you have a specific cocktail bar in mind, message them now (not later).

What time to arrive

For the full evening: 19:00 - 20:00. Eat dinner first, walk in for a full evening of drinks.

For the countdown only: 22:30 - 23:00. Most NYE bookings will not seat walk-ins after 22:30; if you have a reservation, arrive 30 minutes early.

For the post-midnight tail: some bars stay open till 03:00 - 04:00 on NYE only. Both our outlets stretch to 02:00.

The countdown drink question

Most guests want something celebratory in hand at midnight. Options:

French 75 (gin, lemon, sugar, champagne) - sparkling, photogenic, easy to drink at speed if needed.

Champagne, neat - if your bar has decent champagne, this is the classic. Vintage if you can stretch.

Negroni Sbagliato - sparkling Negroni variation. The 2022 internet sensation that still makes sense.

Hibiscus French 75 - our local twist. Pink, photogenic, more interesting than the standard.

Coordinate with the bar in advance. Most will pre-pour champagne for the countdown moment.

What to order before midnight

Pace matters. From a 20:00 arrival to a midnight toast, you have ~4 hours and ~3 drinks of capacity if you also want to actually celebrate at midnight.

A working sequence:

  1. 20:00 - opener. Negroni or a Spritz. Bitter, low ABV, food-friendly.
  2. 21:00 - mid-evening cocktail. Something interesting; a Last Word, a Paper Plane, our local Pandan Collins.
  3. 22:30 - last cocktail before the countdown. Spirit-forward but not too heavy. Old Fashioned or a soft Manhattan.
  4. 00:00 - champagne or French 75. Toast. Drink.
  5. 01:00 onwards - if still going: kopi-led nightcap (Espresso Martini, Kopi Sour, Irish Coffee).

The non-drinker question

Designated drivers and non-drinkers should not be forgotten on NYE. The good bars (ours included) will pour proper mocktails on NYE; some pour a special non-alcoholic spritz with NA sparkling wine for the countdown.

See our piece on a cocktail bar for non-drinkers.

Practical NYE notes

Surge pricing on Grab hits 2-3x normal between 23:00 and 02:00. Plan transport. Some guests pre-book a Grab driver for the return trip.

Parking in PJ is generally easier than KL, even on NYE, because most NYE crowds head to KLCC for fireworks. Damansara Kim and SS2 stay relatively park-able.

Cover charges and minimum spends are common at most KL and PJ cocktail bars on NYE. Expect RM 100-200 per person minimum at popular spots. Both our outlets run a soft minimum spend on NYE; full details available when you reserve.
