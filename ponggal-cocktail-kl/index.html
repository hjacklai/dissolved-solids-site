Ponggal in KL.

The Tamil harvest festival at a Kuala Lumpur cocktail bar. Jaggery, sugarcane, ginger, cardamom. Mixed alcoholic and NA programme, with a quieter Petaling Jaya alternative.

Ponggal is the Tamil harvest festival, celebrated mid-January over four days. It honours the sun, the harvest, and the cattle. The signature dish is sweet rice cooked with jaggery, milk, cardamom, and cashew. At a Kuala Lumpur cocktail bar, the natural translation is jaggery-led, dairy-coded, with sugarcane and ginger as supporting notes.

What to order on Ponggal in KL

Jaggery Old Fashioned: bourbon stirred over jaggery syrup with aromatic bitters.

Sugarcane Daiquiri: white rum, fresh sugarcane juice, lime.

Ginger and Jaggery Highball: any base spirit, fresh ginger syrup, jaggery, soda.

Cardamom Cashew Sour: bourbon, cashew orgeat, cardamom syrup, lemon, egg white.

Pongal Espresso Martini: vodka, espresso, kahlua, jaggery syrup, a small touch of ghee.

Coconut and Cardamom Martini: vodka or gin, coconut cream, cardamom syrup.

For fasting or observing guests (NA programme)

  • NA jaggery cooler: jaggery syrup, lemon, soda.
  • Fresh sugarcane juice: chilled, lime on the side.
  • Ginger and jaggery tea cocktail: brewed ginger tea, jaggery, lemon.
  • Cardamom cashew lassi: yogurt, cardamom, cashew, jaggery.
  • Coconut and cardamom NA: coconut water, cardamom, lime, soda.
  • Iced masala chai with jaggery.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

For a quieter Ponggal evening, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Jaggery and sugarcane on the bar through Ponggal week.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, no printed menu. Tell the bartender it is Ponggal.

The Ponggal evening plan

Daytime: family gatherings, pongal sweet rice, prayers.

Evening, 7pm-10pm: at the bar. Jaggery and sugarcane drinks.
