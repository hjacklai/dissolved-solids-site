International Women's Day in KL.

A small-bar plan for 8 March in Kuala Lumpur. Drinks created by women bartenders, friend-group format, NA programme, and a quieter Petaling Jaya alternative.

International Women's Day on 8 March is one of the year's quietly meaningful cocktail nights. The bar handles it by putting on the classics created by women bartenders, without theatre. No rose-pink lighting, no novelty cocktails. Just the proper Hanky Panky, the proper Sidecar, the proper Margarita.

Drinks created by women bartenders

Hanky Panky: gin, sweet vermouth, Fernet-Branca. Created by Ada Coleman at the American Bar at the Savoy, around 1925.

Sidecar: cognac, Cointreau, lemon. Often credited to Harry's New York Bar in Paris.

Twentieth Century: gin, lemon, Lillet Blanc, white creme de cacao. C. A. Tuck, 1937.

Margarita: tequila, triple sec, lime. Credited to Margarita Sames, 1948.

Cosmopolitan: vodka, Cointreau, cranberry, lime. Modern form refined in the 1980s and 1990s.

What we recommend on 8 March

Hanky Panky, French 75, Lychee Martini, Aviation, Scented Negroni, Roselle Spritz. The drinks should fit the mood, not the colour of the day.

The friend-group format

For 4-8 friends:

  • Dinner first, 7pm-8:30pm.
  • Bar from 9pm, two or three rounds.
  • Out by midnight.

For larger 10-15 person workplace or community groups, brief the bar 5-7 days ahead.

For non-drinking friends

  • NA Hanky Panky.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • Elderflower spritz.
  • Rose Lychee Cooler.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

If central KL is loud or fully booked, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet, conversation-volume.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, no printed menu.
