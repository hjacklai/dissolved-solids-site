What we pour

The bar's short menu rotates seasonally but always covers four categories:

Malaysian-local cocktails. Drinks built on pandan, gula melaka, calamansi, kopi-O, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaf. Our signature is the Kopi Sour: whisky, cold-brewed kopi-O, gula melaka syrup, lemon, foamed. A Malaysian whisky sour with the local-coffee depth.

Classics done seriously. Negroni, Old Fashioned, Manhattan, Boulevardier. Stirred long and cold, served on hand-cut clear ice. No shortcuts.

Coffee cocktails. We took the local kopi-O tradition and ran it through bartending technique. Robusta-based, milk-washed when needed, cold or warm. Read our journal article on why robusta beats arabica for coffee cocktails.

Customised drinks. The bartender will build any shape of drink based on a 30-second chat. No printed list for these, just the conversation. Or use our drink builder on the website, then WhatsApp the result.

Damansara explained: which "Damansara" do you mean?

"Damansara" in PJ refers to a cluster of related but distinct neighbourhoods. If you are searching for a cocktail bar "in Damansara", knowing which one you actually mean saves time:

Damansara Kim. Where Dissolved Solids is. SS20 area, between Damansara Utama and TTDI. Residential, mixed commercial on the SS20 strip. Quiet by PJ standards. Best for: walkable evening drinks, easy parking, residential calm.

Damansara Utama (Uptown PJ). The denser commercial strip immediately east of Damansara Kim. Restaurants, bars, karaoke. Busier on Friday-Saturday evenings. Best for: dinner-then-drinks combinations with multiple venues within walking distance.

Damansara Jaya. South of Damansara Utama, mostly residential, fewer F&B options. Best for: residents looking for a drink without driving far.

Damansara Heights (Bukit Damansara). KL side, embassy belt, upmarket residential. A handful of upscale bars and hotel lounges. Best for: business drinks, expense-account dinners.

Damansara Perdana, Damansara Damai, Bandar Sri Damansara. Newer residential townships further out. Limited bar density. Most residents drive into Damansara Utama or Damansara Kim for cocktail evenings.

Mutiara Damansara. Mall-and-office district near Bandar Utama. Some chain bars and hotel lounges. Not the cocktail-bar destination.

For a serious cocktail evening, the centre of gravity sits in Damansara Kim and Damansara Utama. Both are walkable to each other (10-15 minute walk along Jalan SS20) and most regulars know both venues.

After-drinks options in Damansara

The cocktail evening usually does not end at last call. What is open and useful afterwards:

The 24-hour mamak at the SS20 corner. Roti canai, teh tarik, maggi goreng. The Malaysian late-night classic. Walking distance from Dissolved Solids.

The Indian Muslim restaurant a block north. Open till 02:00 most nights. Nasi kandar, curry rice. Heavier than mamak but a proper meal.

The 24-hour McDonald's at Damansara Utama. A 5-minute drive from Damansara Kim. For the late, casual end-of-evening.

Dim sum on Sunday morning at Damansara Utama. Several spots open from 06:30. Useful if you over-stayed last call on Saturday and want a recovery breakfast.

FAQ: Visiting a Damansara cocktail bar

Is Damansara Kim safe at night? Yes. It is a residential neighbourhood with the standard urban awareness. Street lighting is good on the main commercial strip. Most regulars walk from car-park to bar without concern.

Where do I park? Free street parking on Jalan SS20/11 after 18:00. Covered municipal lot on Jalan SS20/10 (~RM 2/hour, almost always has space). For peak Friday/Saturday after 20:00, plan to walk a block from your spot.

Can I walk between Damansara Kim and Damansara Utama? Yes. 10-15 minute walk along Jalan SS20. Some regulars do dinner in Damansara Utama then walk to Dissolved Solids for drinks.

What time should I come? Tuesday-Thursday 19:00-21:00 is quietest and best for conversation. Friday/Saturday 20:00 onwards is busiest. Sunday afternoon (15:00-17:00) is our favourite shift for the unhurried drinker.

Are there other serious cocktail bars in Damansara? Yes, a few. The neighbourhood has matured into a small cocktail district. We will not name competitors here (we leave them to introduce themselves), but ask any of our bartenders for recommendations, the Damansara cocktail community is collaborative.

Can I bring kids? Children welcome at our bar before 20:00. After that the rooms get smokier and the music goes up.
