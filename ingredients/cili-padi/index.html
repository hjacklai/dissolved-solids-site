Cili padi.

Capsicum frutescens. Bird's eye chilli. The small Malaysian fire that punches way above its size. In cocktails, it brings clean heat that wakes the palate without lingering bitterness.

Cili padi is the Malaysian cocktail ingredient that gets used least but works best. A single sliced chilli in the shaker transforms a Margarita or a Whisky Sour. The heat is clean and citrus-like, not muddy. Used in tiny quantities, it amplifies the rest of the build instead of dominating it.

What it is

Capsicum frutescens. Native to Central and South America, naturalised across Southeast Asia. The small (2-3cm) red or green chillies are called cili padi (Malay), thai chilli, bird's eye chilli, or piri piri. Heat rating: 50,000 to 100,000 Scoville Heat Units. Hot but not extreme.

Flavour profile

Clean heat with a faint citrus-grass top note. Less smoky than jalapeño, less floral than habanero. Pairs naturally with tequila, mezcal, white rum, gin, vodka. Companion ingredients: lime, lemongrass, ginger, kaffir lime leaf, mango, pineapple, cucumber, salt.

Where to source cili padi in Malaysia

Wet markets: RM 4 to 12 per 100g (depending on season). Year-round.

Supermarkets: any Malaysian supermarket. Look for firm bright-red or bright-green chillies. Soft or shrivelled means past prime.

Heat note: green cili padi is typically slightly hotter than red of the same size. The heat sits in the seeds and membranes; deseed for milder use.

How to prep cili padi for cocktails

Sliced infusion (the standard). Slice 1 chilli into 3-4 rings, drop into the shaker before adding ice. Strain out. The cocktail picks up the heat without the visual.

Cili padi syrup. 250ml water, 200g sugar, 4-6 sliced chillies. Simmer 10 minutes, taste, strain when the heat is right. Keeps refrigerated 3 weeks.

Chilli-honey syrup. Replace sugar with honey in the cili padi syrup. The chilli-honey pair is one of the great cocktail combinations.

Chilli-infused tequila. 6 sliced chillies in 500ml tequila. Cap, leave 4 to 6 hours, taste, strain. The tequila picks up clean heat throughout.

Best cocktails with cili padi

Cili Padi Margarita: tequila, fresh lime, triple sec, chilli-honey syrup, salt rim.

Spicy Paloma: tequila, fresh grapefruit, lime, soda, chilli-infused syrup.

Chilli-Mango Margarita: tequila, mango puree, lime, chilli syrup. Sweet-hot tropical.

Tom Yum Cocktail: vodka, lime, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaf, cili padi, fish sauce. Thai street-food in a glass.

Chilli-Honey Whisky Sour: bourbon, fresh lemon, chilli-honey syrup, egg white.

In Malaysian use

Cili padi is a naturalised Malaysian ingredient. The plant originated in the Americas (introduced by Portuguese traders through Melaka in the 16th century) but it has been here so long that it is genuinely the local chilli, woven into almost every Malay, Peranakan, Indian-Muslim, and Chinese-Malaysian preparation. It is the base of sambal belacan, the heat in laksa and nasi lemak, the bite of cincalok, the fire in kerabu salads, and the green chilli in mamak teh tarik shop chutney. Most Malaysian kitchens grow a cili padi plant on a windowsill.

For sourcing, the freshest cili padi is at any wet market (TTDI, Section 17, Pudu, Chow Kit, Selayang) at RM 4 to 12 per 100g. Supermarkets stock smaller packs at slightly higher per-gram prices. At the bar, our local riff is a cili padi tincture paired with calamansi and gula melaka in a Margarita variant, where the three local ingredients land together in a way that feels closer to a Penang rojak than to a Cancun resort. Locally we also see cili padi infusions in mamak-stall mocktails and in many home kitchens' herbal vinegars.

Substitutions

  • Jalapeño for a milder, more vegetal-grassy substitute.
  • Tabasco / hot sauce for a flavour-shortcut (different character but similar heat function).
  • Pink peppercorn for a non-chilli alternative source of heat.

Frequently asked questions

What does cili padi taste like in cocktails?

Cili padi (bird's eye chilli) delivers clean heat with a faint citrus-grass top note, less smoky than jalapeno and less floral than habanero. The heat is bright and short-finishing, not muddy. Used in tiny quantities it amplifies the rest of the build instead of dominating. Pairs naturally with tequila, mezcal, white rum, gin, lime, lemongrass, and mango.

Where can I buy cili padi in Malaysia?

Wet markets are the best value: RM 4 to 12 per 100g at TTDI market, Section 17 PJ, Pudu, Chow Kit, Selayang, and Pasar Borong Selangor. Supermarkets (Cold Storage, Jaya Grocer, Village Grocer, Mydin, AEON) stock smaller packs at slightly higher per-gram prices. Green cili padi is slightly hotter than red of the same size.

What can I substitute for cili padi?

Jalapeno gives a milder, more vegetal-grassy substitute. Tabasco or any clean hot sauce is a flavour-shortcut with similar heat function but different aromatic character. Pink peppercorn is a non-chilli alternative with floral heat. Thai bird's eye chilli is essentially the same species; use interchangeably.

Which cocktails use cili padi?

Cili Padi Margarita (tequila, lime, triple sec, chilli-honey syrup, salt rim), Spicy Paloma, Chilli-Mango Margarita, Tom Yum Cocktail (vodka, lime, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaf, cili padi, fish sauce), and the Chilli-Honey Whisky Sour. Our local riff pairs cili padi tincture with calamansi and gula melaka in a Margarita variant.

How long does cili padi keep?

Fresh cili padi keeps 1 week at room temperature, 2 weeks refrigerated, 6 months frozen whole. Dried cili padi lasts 12 months in an airtight jar. Cili padi syrup keeps 3 weeks refrigerated. Chilli-infused tequila is shelf-stable indefinitely once strained. Wear gloves when slicing; the oils linger on your hands for hours.
