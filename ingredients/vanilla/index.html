Vanilla.

Vanilla planifolia. The seed pod of a Mexican orchid, fermented and dried. The most universally loved sweet aromatic on earth. In cocktails, it bridges almost everything sweet to almost everything spirited.

Vanilla is the cocktail ingredient that quietly improves almost any sweet build. A scraped half-pod in a syrup. A bar spoon of high-quality extract in a sour. A pod-infused rum. Vanilla works because vanillin, its main flavour molecule, sits at the centre of human "sweetness" perception.

What it is

Vanilla planifolia. A climbing orchid native to Mexico. The seed pod (vanilla bean) is harvested green, cured, and dried over months to develop the characteristic flavour. Madagascar (Bourbon vanilla) is the dominant producer; Tahitian vanilla (Vanilla tahitensis) is more floral; Mexican vanilla is rarer.

Vanilla forms for cocktails

  • Whole bean: the best for cocktails. Split, scrape seeds, use both seeds and pod.
  • Vanilla extract: seeds steeped in alcohol. Good for syrups, infusions, baking. Buy "pure vanilla extract", not "vanilla flavouring" (which is synthetic).
  • Vanilla paste: seeds suspended in syrup. Convenient for cocktail use, more concentrated than extract.
  • Vanilla syrup: pre-made, varies in quality. Read the label.

Flavour profile

Sweet, floral, creamy, with a warm round finish. Pairs naturally with: bourbon, rum, brandy, cognac, sake, anything dessert-coded. Companion ingredients: cardamom, cinnamon, chocolate, coffee, citrus, honey, gula melaka, butter.

Where to source vanilla in Malaysia

Whole pods: Cold Storage, Mercato, BIG, Bens stock Madagascar vanilla pods. RM 15 to 30 per pod.

Extract: any baking supply or Indian grocer.

Quality marker: pods should be plump, dark, slightly flexible, with a strong sweet aroma. Dry brittle pods are old.

How to prep vanilla for cocktails

Vanilla simple syrup. Split 1 vanilla bean lengthwise, scrape seeds. Combine with 250ml water and 200g sugar. Simmer 5 minutes, take off heat, leave to steep with pod and seeds for 30 minutes. Strain (or leave the bean in for ongoing infusion). Keeps refrigerated 3 weeks.

Vanilla-infused bourbon. 1 split pod in 500ml bourbon. Cap, leave 5 days, taste, strain.

Vanilla salt rim. Scraped vanilla seeds + sea salt + sugar. Use as a Margarita or sour rim.

Tahitian vanilla extract. Brew Tahitian for fruit-floral builds; Madagascar for caramel-richer builds. The cocktail will be different.

Best cocktails with vanilla

Vanilla Old Fashioned: bourbon, vanilla syrup, aromatic bitters, large rock.

Vanilla Daiquiri: aged rum, fresh lime, vanilla syrup. Tropical-coded.

Vanilla-Pear Martini: vodka, fresh pear, vanilla syrup, lemon.

Coffee-Vanilla Espresso Martini: vodka, espresso, kahlua, vanilla syrup.

Cardamom-Vanilla Hot Toddy: bourbon, hot water, vanilla, cardamom, honey, lemon.

Substitutions

  • Vanilla extract for vanilla pod (about 1 teaspoon extract = 1 pod).
  • Vanilla paste for extract (more concentrated, equal parts).
  • Tonka bean for a more aromatic-complex alternative (use sparingly).
