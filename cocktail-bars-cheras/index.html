Cocktail bar near Cheras.

Cheras has a strong food scene but a thin cocktail-bar scene. The closest serious cocktail bars are 30-35 minutes west in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Cheras is a large eastern-KL township with serious food culture (the bak kut teh, the Hakka noodles, the Cantonese seafood) but a thin cocktail-bar scene relative to the size of the area. For Cheras residents who want a proper cocktail evening, the 30-35 minute drive west into PJ is often the most reliable option.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times from Cheras

  • Taman Connaught: 32 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 35 to Soluble Solids.
  • Sungai Long: 35 minutes / 38.
  • Alam Damai: 33 minutes / 36.
  • Pandan Indah: 28 minutes / 32.
  • Bandar Tun Razak: 30 minutes / 33.
  • Leisure Mall area: 32 minutes / 35.

Route: SMART tunnel or the MRR2 (Middle Ring Road 2) heading west, then onto the LDP. Grab fares: RM 30-45 to either bar.

Why drive 30 minutes west

The honest answer: the eastern-KL cocktail-bar scene has not developed at the same pace as PJ or central KL. The KL-side options closer to Cheras are mostly lounge-format or club-format venues; dedicated cocktail bars in the strict sense are rare.

For a proper cocktail evening with hand-cut ice, fresh-squeezed citrus, house-made syrups, and a bartender who will customise the build, the PJ corridor is the closest reliable answer for Cheras residents. The 30-minute drive feels longer in your head than on the road, especially via the SMART tunnel after 19:00.
