Cocktail bar near Cyberjaya.

Cyberjaya and Putrajaya have very thin cocktail-bar scenes by design. The closest serious cocktail bars are 35-40 minutes north in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Cyberjaya was built as a tech corridor, and Putrajaya as a government administrative city. Both are residential and increasingly populated, but neither has a developed cocktail-bar scene. For Cyberjaya tech workers and Putrajaya residents who want a serious cocktail evening, the 35-40 minute drive north into PJ is the standard option.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times

  • Cyberjaya tech park area: 35 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 38 to Soluble Solids.
  • Multimedia University (MMU): 38 / 40.
  • Putrajaya central: 40 minutes / 42.
  • Dengkil: 40 / 42.
  • Bangi (KL-Putrajaya highway side): 35 / 38.

Route: ELITE Highway or Maju Expressway north, then onto the LDP. Mostly highway-grade road.

Grab fares from Cyberjaya: RM 40-58 to either bar. Surge multipliers rare except after 22:00 on Friday and Saturday.

For after-work tech evenings

If you are leaving the office in Cyberjaya at 18:30 and want a cocktail before driving home: Dissolved Solids opens at 15:00 on weekdays, so the room is calm when you arrive around 19:15. Stay for two drinks, leave by 21:30, home by 22:15. The rhythm works for a midweek decompression evening.

For weekend evenings

Both bars are at their best Friday or Saturday from 19:30. The 40-minute drive from Putrajaya/Cyberjaya is the largest fixed cost in the evening; most weekend visitors plan a 4-5 hour visit to make it worth it.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Sun, opens 14:00 or 15:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun, 18:00-01:00.

Grab home after drinks. The PJ-to-Cyberjaya pickup is usually quick.
