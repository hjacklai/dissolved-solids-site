Snacks for the long sit.

Three snacks we serve alongside cocktails. For when one drink turns into two and you start eyeing the kitchen.

Chicken Nuggets

Hand-formed chicken pieces, breaded and fried hot. Built to share. Our most-ordered with a Negroni.

Crispy Broccoli

Broccoli florets fried until the edges crisp, salted hot. Surprisingly the snack people come back for. Pairs with the Scented Negroni.

Popcorn Chicken

Bite-sized chicken pieces with a crisp coating. Best with the coffee cocktails (Too Gouda, Peach Blossom).

Snacks at Dissolved Solids exist for one reason: a long table needs a long table. The kitchen is small, deliberately. We focus on a tight set of snacks that go with the drinks instead of competing with them.

Everything is fried fresh after you order, so there's a 5 to 10 minute wait. If you're coming with friends and plan to order food, we recommend ordering snacks before drinks so they arrive in roughly the same window.

Frequently asked questions

What is the snack menu like at Dissolved Solids?

The menu is deliberately tight: chicken nuggets, crispy broccoli, and popcorn chicken. All three are fried fresh after you order, all three are built to share, and all three pair with the cocktail list rather than competing with it. The kitchen at Damansara Kim is small by design; we focus on a few items done well rather than a long menu of compromises.

Are the snacks pork-free?

Yes. The snack menu at Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim is pork-free. Chicken nuggets and popcorn chicken use chicken; crispy broccoli is plant-based. Alcohol is served, so the bar is not halal-certified, but pork is not used in any snack. If a guest in your group is observing strict halal rules around alcohol-adjacent food, mention it when you arrive and we will guide the order.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Crispy broccoli is the vegetarian and vegan option (florets fried until the edges crisp, salted hot). It is also, somewhat unexpectedly, the most ordered snack of the three. Chicken nuggets and popcorn chicken both contain chicken. If you need a fully vegetarian table, order extra crispy broccoli and we can size the portion up; tell the bartender when you arrive.

Do you have allergen information?

All three snacks are fried in the same oil, so cross-contact between items is possible. Chicken nuggets and popcorn chicken contain wheat (breading) and chicken. Crispy broccoli contains wheat (light coating) and is fried in shared oil. No nuts are used in the snack menu itself. For severe allergies (gluten, shellfish, dairy), tell the bartender on arrival and we will confirm what is safe that day.

Can I have a full meal at Dissolved Solids?

No. The snacks are designed to extend a drinking sit, not replace dinner. For a full meal before drinks, Damansara Kim has Table & Apron, Kompassion, and Piccoli Lotti within a 5-minute walk. Eat first, walk over, then order snacks here to carry you through cocktails. Soluble Solids in SS2 does not currently serve any snacks; that outlet is drinks-only.
