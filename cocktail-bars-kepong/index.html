Cocktail bar near Kepong.

From Kepong, Desa Park City, or Manjalara to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 22-28 minutes by car. The closest serious cocktail bars to the Kepong residential area.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Kepong is a large residential belt north of Petaling Jaya, running from Kepong Baru and Desa Park City through Manjalara, Sri Damansara, and Bandar Menjalara. Dense with food, especially Chinese-Malaysian cooking, but light on dedicated cocktail bars. For Kepong residents who want a serious cocktail evening, the 15 to 28 minute drive south is the standard answer.

Our two bars from Kepong

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Open Wednesday to Sunday, 18:00 to 01:00. Maps · WhatsApp +60 11-1682 8651

From most of Kepong the drive south on the LDP or MRR2 is a short, predictable run. From Desa Park City to Dissolved Solids it is 15 to 20 minutes door to door, the kind of distance that makes a 90-minute weekday date-night format easy. From Kepong Baru and the older blocks the drive extends to 24 to 28 minutes, still well within the comfort zone for a midweek evening. The route is mostly highway-grade with two or three traffic lights at the PJ end where the LDP feeds into Damansara Utama or Section 17.

The character of Kepong

Kepong has three different feels stitched together. Kepong Baru and Taman Kepong are the older Chinese-Malaysian residential blocks, with long-running family restaurants, dim sum at sunrise, and a strong claypot rice and bak kut teh tradition. Desa Park City and Manjalara are the newer mid-to-upper-tier gated developments, with walkable parks, dog-friendly cafés, and a steadily growing F and B scene. Sri Damansara and Bandar Menjalara sit between, more commercial-residential, dense with kopitiams and Chinese tai-chow restaurants.

FRIM (the Forest Research Institute of Malaysia) borders Kepong's north edge, giving the area more green space than most KL suburbs. The Kepong Metropolitan Park lake and Kepong Botanic Garden pull weekend joggers and cyclists. Food markets along Jalan Kepong run from morning hawker stalls to late-night supper rows.

Desa Park City has a few notable cocktail venues, generally lounge or restaurant-bar shaped, oriented to the upmarket family crowd. Kepong Baru and the older blocks have very few dedicated cocktail bars; the local drinking culture there leans toward Chinese restaurant beer and casual gastropubs.

The Kepong family-condo demographic shapes the evening rhythm here. A typical Kepong Friday night is a family-format dinner (claypot at Kepong Baru, dim sum brunch run long at Desa Park City, char kway teow at Sri Damansara) followed by an early-to-mid evening. The 90-minute date-night format is the dominant pattern: couples with children at home arrange a babysitter or a parent-stay, leave the house at 19:15, arrive at the bar by 19:35, two drinks across 90 minutes, and home before 22:00. The compactness of the round trip from Desa Park City makes this format work consistently. By contrast, weekend evenings tilt toward longer-format four-hour visits with friends rather than couples, often starting after a family lunch with extended family.

Why drive south for the drink

A small specialist cocktail bar needs the kind of density of dinner restaurants and walkable parking that PJ's older commercial corridors (SS2, Damansara Kim, Section 17) provide. The Kepong area is generally too residential, with parking and pedestrian patterns oriented to family homes rather than evening drinkers. Crossing the MRR2 south puts you in that PJ format within 20 to 25 minutes.

A typical Kepong-resident move is a long Chinese family dinner (claypot at Kepong Baru, dim sum brunch at Desa Park City, char kway teow at Sri Damansara) and then driving south to PJ for one or two well-made cocktails before heading home. The drive each way is short enough to make this normal on a Friday or Saturday.

The cleanest route is the MRR2 south to either the Damansara Utama exit (for Dissolved Solids) or Section 17 (for Soluble Solids). Off-peak the drive is straight highway. The DUKE Expressway is the alternative from the Kepong Baru side: DUKE eastbound, then south on the LDP. Slightly longer in distance but usually less stop-and-go traffic.

Friday evening 18:00 to 19:30, the MRR2 south can slow by 8 to 12 minutes; many regulars time their drive for 19:30 onwards or before 17:30. Saturday afternoon is the cleanest window: 18 to 22 minutes door to door from most of Kepong.

Grab fares from Kepong: RM 25 to 38 to either bar.

Signature drinks worth the drive

The Kepong-to-PJ drive is short enough that the order on first arrival can be exploratory: try a signature without worrying about wasting the trip. The house signatures at Dissolved Solids cluster around Malaysian ingredients built with classical bar technique. Start with the Kopi Sour (whisky, freshly brewed kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon, egg-white foam): an opener that reads as both familiar and unmistakably local. Then the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan leaf, lime, soda), which works as a clean palate reset after a Chinese family dinner. The Gula Melaka Old Fashioned swaps demerara for Malaysian palm sugar, the caramel matching wok-cooked food well. The Calamansi Highball is the lightest option, suited to a 90-minute date-night second drink. The Scented Negroni is the bitter-led pick, recently refreshed with a local botanical layer. The Local Tropic is the lightest tropical of the menu.

At Soluble Solids the format is different: there is no printed list. Tell the bartender three short answers (strength, spirit you usually reach for, flavour direction) and the drink is built in front of you from the wall of labelled infusion jars. The flagship build is The Tesseract, a multi-spirit signature treatment the bar pours as a counter-experience for guests who order "surprise me." After a long Kepong Cantonese lunch, "something light, gin, citrusy" tends to land well as an opener.

Parking on arrival

Parking is one of the better-kept advantages of the PJ format for a Kepong driver, especially for the date-night format where a quick clean parking experience matters. Dissolved Solids sits inside the Damansara Kim grid, a low-rise residential and shoplot block where free street parking runs the length of Jalan SS20/11 and the surrounding side roads. Tuesday or Wednesday evenings you will usually find a slot directly outside; Friday and Saturday after 20:00 expect a one to three minute walk from a slot in the adjacent residential row. No fee, no boom-gate.

Soluble Solids sits on the SS2 row near the wet market and food court. Free street parking is available along Jalan SS2/24 and the adjacent SS2 lanes. The SS2 wet-market public lot two minutes' walk away is the reliable overflow after 19:00 when the row fills. From a Kepong family-driver's perspective the parking calculus is simple: easier than Mont Kiara, easier than Bangsar, and easier than KLCC.

Typical evening expectations

Both rooms are small (around 40 to 50 seats) and operate at conversation-level volume rather than club-volume. The music sits at a low-mid level: vinyl and curated playlists at Dissolved Solids leaning toward bossa, jazz and dub; Soluble Solids runs a more eclectic mix sometimes including slowed Mandopop or local indie. Neither room has a dance floor and neither operates a guest-list or a queue. The lighting is warm and low rather than dim-and-moody.

For a Kepong couple stepping out for the 90-minute date-night format, the contrast is the appeal: a quieter, slower room where conversation lands and the drink is properly built. The bar guests around you will be a mix of PJ residents, Damansara couples, post-dinner groups of four or six, and a steady flow of cross-town visitors from Mont Kiara and the northern suburbs. Conversation is the dominant register; you should be able to hear the person across the table without raising your voice. The typical seated stay is 90 to 120 minutes for two to three drinks, suiting the Kepong date-night rhythm well.

Reservations and walk-ins

Dissolved Solids: Tuesday to Sunday, opens 14:00 or 15:00 depending on day, closes 00:00 (Tue-Thu, Sun) or 01:00 (Fri-Sat). Closed Mondays. WhatsApp +60 11-4008 7607.

Soluble Solids: Wednesday to Sunday, 18:00 to 01:00. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays. WhatsApp +60 11-1682 8651.

Walk-ins are welcome at both bars; weeknights before 21:00 there is almost always a seat. For Friday and Saturday after 21:00, or for any group of six or more, WhatsApp ahead and the team will hold a table. The WhatsApp channel is not only for reservations: questions about a specific spirit, a drink you tasted elsewhere and want recreated, or a dietary or non-alcoholic requirement are all welcomed in advance.

Getting back to Kepong after

The drive back via the MRR2 is fast post-midnight, 15 to 22 minutes with no traffic. Police checkpoints occasionally operate on the MRR2 Friday and Saturday nights; never drive after a drink. Grab is reliable for the return at 01:00 but surge-priced weekends (budget RM 35 to 55 back). The MRT Putrajaya line stations in Kepong (Kepong Baru, Metro Prima, Kentonmen) stop running before last call, so they are not a return option at that hour.

What cocktail pairs well with a long Chinese family dinner from Kepong?

Is parking easy after the drive from Kepong?

Is the 90-minute date-night format possible from Desa Park City?
