Cocktail bar near Selayang.

From Selayang, Batu Caves, or Templer Park to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 28-35 minutes by car. The closest serious cocktail bars to the Selayang area.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Selayang is a large mixed residential and commercial area in the northern Klang Valley, famous for its wholesale market and food. The cocktail-bar scene immediately around Selayang is thin; for a proper cocktail evening the standard option is to drive south to PJ.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times

  • Selayang town centre: 30 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 33 to Soluble Solids.
  • Batu Caves: 32 minutes / 35.
  • Templer Park: 35 minutes / 38.
  • Rawang: 40 minutes / 42.

Route: take the MRR2 or LATAR Expressway south, join the LDP. Mostly highway road.

Grab fares from Selayang: RM 32-50 to either bar.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Sun, opens 14:00 or 15:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun, 18:00-01:00.
