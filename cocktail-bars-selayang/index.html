Selayang is a large mixed residential and commercial area in the northern Klang Valley, famous for its wholesale market and food. The cocktail-bar scene immediately around Selayang is thin; for a proper cocktail evening the standard option is to drive south to PJ.

Selayang, the wholesale-market evening

Selayang runs on a schedule most of KL never sees. The Pasar Borong Selayang, the wholesale produce market that supplies half the Klang Valley, wakes up around 02:00 and is winding down by sunrise. Lorry drivers, market porters, restaurant buyers, and fishmongers form a separate population working an inverted clock, and the food scene around Jalan Ipoh Lama and Jalan Selayang reflects it, twenty-four-hour ban mian shops, lok-lok stalls open until 04:00, fish-head curry places that serve breakfast crowds at 06:00. This is not a cocktail-bar demographic, and that is reasonable; the people doing the actual work are clocking off when most bars are still pouring last call.

The cocktail audience in Selayang sits a layer above the market workers, in the residential blocks of Rawang, Sungai Buloh fringe, the new condo developments around the MRT line, and the family terrace houses of Bandar Baru Selayang. For these households, a Friday-night cocktail evening is firmly a planned outing rather than a habit, the Selayang and Rawang side of the Klang Valley sits outside the usual KL nightlife radius. Driving south on the MRR2 or the LATAR Expressway lands you in PJ in roughly half an hour, and the route is dead simple, two highways, one merge, no tolls of any consequence past 21:00. The crowd we see making this drive is typically four-person tables, friends or partnered couples, leaving the Selayang Mall area around 19:30 and arriving as the room hits its evening pace.
