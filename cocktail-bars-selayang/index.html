Cocktail bar near Selayang.

From Selayang, Batu Caves, or Templer Park to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 28-35 minutes by car. The closest serious cocktail bars to the Selayang area.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Selayang is a large mixed residential and commercial belt in the northern Klang Valley, running roughly from Jalan Ipoh out toward Templer Park and Rawang. Famous for its wholesale market and food culture, the area has a thin cocktail-bar scene. For Selayang residents who want a proper cocktail evening, the 28 to 35 minute drive south into PJ is the standard answer.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

The character of Selayang

Most people who live around Bandar Baru Selayang, Taman Selayang Mulia, or Selayang Heights know the area as a food destination first. The Selayang wholesale market is the largest fresh-produce hub in the northern Klang Valley. The bak kut teh chains along Jalan Ipoh are some of the busiest in the country. The Indian Muslim coffee shops near Selayang Hospital pull a steady local crowd from early morning until late.

What Selayang does not have is a developed dedicated cocktail-bar scene. The structural reason is land use: the surrounding zoning is wholesale, light industrial, and suburban housing, none of which produces the pedestrian density that a serious cocktail bar needs to survive. A small specialist room with 50 seats has to fill those seats five nights a week with people who walk in or drive a short distance. That model works in PJ and central KL, not in Selayang.

Why drive south for the drink

A serious cocktail bar requires three things you cannot bootstrap quickly: fresh-cut citrus prepped daily (not bottled juice), a back bar of 60 or more bottles with depth in vermouth, bitters, and amari, and a bartender who can taste through your drink mid-build and adjust. Petaling Jaya's smaller owner-operated rooms have all three. Selayang's hotel-bar and karaoke-lounge venues mostly do not.

The typical Selayang move is to eat local (bak kut teh on Jalan Ipoh, claypot rice, or a kopitiam breakfast) and then drive south for the drink. Twenty-eight to thirty-five minutes is short enough that this is realistic on a weekday evening.

Drive times

  • Selayang town centre: 30 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 33 to Soluble Solids.
  • Bandar Baru Selayang: 28 minutes / 31.
  • Taman Selayang Mulia: 30 minutes / 33.
  • Selayang Heights: 32 minutes / 35.
  • Batu Caves: 32 minutes / 35.
  • Templer Park (Rawang side): 35 minutes / 38.
  • Rawang town: 40 minutes / 42.

From Bandar Baru Selayang or Taman Selayang Mulia, the cleanest route is the MRR2 westbound, then the LDP south. Exit Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, or continue one more interchange south to Section 17 for Soluble Solids. Off-peak the drive is straight motorway, no traffic lights once you join the MRR2. Friday and Saturday after 19:00 adds 10 to 15 minutes; a common workaround is to arrive at the bar between 18:00 and 19:00 rather than 20:00 and 21:00.

From Batu Caves the route is the same with 4 to 6 extra minutes. From Templer Park or Rawang, take the LATAR Expressway south, exit Sungai Buloh, join the LDP. About 38 to 42 minutes door to door.

Grab fares from the wider Selayang area: RM 32 to 50 to either bar, depending on time of day. Returning home post-midnight is usually surge-priced; budget RM 50 to 70 for the trip back.

What to order if it is your first time

At Dissolved Solids, ask the bartender for either the Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, foamed) or the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan, lime, soda). Both demonstrate what a Malaysian-local cocktail can be when done with restraint. If you want something more classic, the Negroni here arrives with a clean spirit-forward profile and a single large clear cube of ice, not crushed.

At Soluble Solids, skip ordering by name. Tell the bartender three words: strength (light, medium, strong), spirit preference (gin, rum, whisky, mezcal, surprise), and flavour profile (citrusy, herbal, smoky, sweet). They build the drink from the infusion shelf, which is the same wall of fruit, leaf, and herb glass jars you see when you walk in. No printed list.

Hours and when to come

Dissolved Solids: Tuesday to Sunday, opens 14:00 or 15:00 depending on day. Closes 00:00 (Tue-Thu, Sun) or 01:00 (Fri-Sat). Closed Mondays.

Soluble Solids: Wednesday to Sunday, 18:00 to 01:00. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

Dissolved Solids is the better fit if you are coming straight from a late lunch in Selayang or want a quieter afternoon drink. Soluble Solids works better for the evening room where you settle in for two or three drinks rather than a quick stop. Walk-ins are fine at both. WhatsApp ahead for Friday/Saturday after 21:00 or any group of six or more.

Getting back to Selayang after

The drive back to Selayang is fast post-midnight: 18 to 22 minutes via the LDP and MRR2, with no traffic. Police checkpoints occasionally operate on the MRR2 and LDP, especially Friday and Saturday nights; never drive if you have had a drink. Grab is universal and reliable for the return trip, even at 01:00.

If you would rather not drive back, both bars are about 8 to 10 minutes from Taman Bahagia LRT (the Kelana Jaya line). From there it is a 25 minute LRT ride to KL Sentral, then a Grab onward.
