Cocktail bar near Putrajaya.

Putrajaya itself has almost no cocktail-bar scene by design. The closest serious cocktail bars are 40-45 minutes north in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Putrajaya was planned as a federal administrative city, and the F&B scene reflects that — mostly restaurants and cafés, very few licensed bars. For Putrajaya residents and federal-government workers who want a serious cocktail evening, the 40-45 minute drive north into Petaling Jaya is the standard answer.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids — Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids — SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times from Putrajaya

  • Putrajaya Sentral / Presint 1: 40 minutes off-peak.
  • Presint 8 / Presint 11: 42 minutes.
  • IOI Resort City area: 38 minutes.
  • Putrajaya Lake / Iron Mosque: 40 minutes.
  • Cyberjaya tech park (very near Putrajaya): 35 minutes.

Route: ELITE Highway northbound or the Maju Expressway, joining the LDP at Damansara. Mostly highway, fast outside peak.

Grab fares: RM 45-60 to either bar.

Worth the trip?

The 40-minute drive is the longest fixed cost on this list, but for Putrajaya residents most weekends still pencil out. A 4-hour evening with friends at a proper cocktail bar in PJ beats sitting in the few options closer to Putrajaya, in our (admittedly biased) experience. The drive home off-peak is similarly fast.

For after-work Friday evenings: leave the office at 18:30, arrive at the bar 19:15, stay until 22:00, home by 23:00. The maths works.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Sun, opens 14:00 or 15:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun, 18:00-01:00.

Grab home after drinks. Pickup wait from PJ to Putrajaya is usually 8-12 minutes off-peak.
