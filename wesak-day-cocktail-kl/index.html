Wesak Day in KL.

A respectful, non-alcoholic-focused cocktail-bar plan for Wesak Day in Kuala Lumpur. Why we lean NA on this day, a full mocktail line-up, and a Petaling Jaya alternative.

Wesak Day commemorates the birth, enlightenment, and parinirvana of the Buddha. Many Buddhists across Malaysia observe the Five Precepts on this day, which includes refraining from intoxicants. We respect that. Our Wesak Day programme leans entirely toward the NA side: serious mocktails, proper coffee, brewed tea cocktails, everything alcohol-free, served in the same glassware with the same care as the regular menu.

What "NA-focused" means at a craft cocktail bar

A serious NA cocktail menu is not "the soda with lime garnish" workaround. It is a real menu. The bartender builds the drink with the same technique: shake, stir, dilute, double-strain, garnish, present. The only thing that changes is the spirit.

On Wesak Day we run the NA programme as the default for guests who specify they are observing. We have the alcohol menu available as usual for guests who are not, but we will not push it. If you have come specifically for a quiet, mindful evening, the bar will respect that.

What to order on Wesak Day

The Wesak menu leans toward tea, herbs, and warm spices. Calm flavours, considered presentations.

Lotus Tea Cooler: brewed lotus flower tea, lemon, honey, ice. Wesak's signature flower in liquid form.

Jasmine White Tea Spritz: jasmine tea and white tea blend, lemon, soda. Floral, clean.

Osmanthus and Honey Cooler: osmanthus tea, honey, lemon, ice. Quiet, balanced.

Sandalwood Mocktail: sandalwood-infused syrup (yes, sandalwood is edible in tiny quantities and is a Wesak-coded flavour), lemon, soda.

Pandan and Coconut Refresher: pandan syrup, coconut water, lime, soda. Malaysian-local and entirely NA.

Sirap Selasih: rose syrup with soaked basil seeds. Traditional, beloved by older Malaysian Buddhists.

Iced Lemongrass and Ginger: brewed lemongrass tea, fresh ginger, honey, ice.

Kombucha-by-the-glass: when a fresh batch is ready.

Coffee and tea programmes

On Wesak we also lean into the coffee and tea side of the bar (we run both seriously, year-round, but Wesak is when they get the most attention):

  • Cold-brewed kopi-O with gula melaka, in a coupe
  • Aged Japanese green tea (kabusecha), served brewed
  • Brewed jasmine tea, hot or iced, served in proper glassware
  • Cold-brewed white tea with lemon and honey
  • Espresso-and-condensed-milk Vietnamese-style
  • Single-origin pour-overs (we keep a small espresso programme; on Wesak we lean into the pour-over)

The Petaling Jaya alternative

If you want a quieter, more contemplative room for Wesak Day, PJ is 15-25 minutes from KL central. Our outlets are both small (~30 seats), with conversation-volume music and minimal theatre.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; on Wesak we keep music slightly lower than usual.

Soluble Solids · SS2: the smaller of the two outlets. No printed menu. On Wesak we run the NA programme by default.

The Wesak evening plan

Morning to afternoon, temple visits: the actual observance. Dāna offerings, the bath of the Buddha statues, listening to dharma talks.

Vegetarian dinner, 6pm-8pm: a proper vegetarian Chinese or Indian meal.

NA cocktail bar visit, 8pm-10pm: the bar with the serious mocktail programme. Tea-led drinks, the lotus tea cooler, the osmanthus honey.

If the evening runs late: a coffee mocktail or a proper pour-over. The bar will run as long as you want to sit.

For guests who are not observing

If your group is mixed (some observing Wesak, some not), the bar handles both. The bartender will pour the alcoholic version for one guest and the NA version for another, in the same glassware, at the same time. We do not announce "this is the alcoholic one and this is not". The glasses look identical.

Reservations

Wesak Day is a public holiday but not a high-traffic bar night. Walk-ins generally work. For groups of 4 or more, WhatsApp ahead.
