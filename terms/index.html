Terms, briefly.

A short, honest set of terms for visiting our bars and using this website. Written in normal English. If something doesn't make sense, ask.

Last updated 16 May 2026

About this site

dissolvedsolids.co is operated by the team behind Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim) and Soluble Solids (SS2 Petaling Jaya). The information on the site is provided in good faith but isn't guaranteed to be accurate at every moment. Hours, menu items, and contact details can change. If something looks off, the bar's WhatsApp number is the most reliable source.

Reservations

Reservations are made via WhatsApp. A booking is confirmed when we send back a confirmation message, not when you submit the form. We'll usually confirm within an hour during open hours.

We don't take deposits and we don't charge cancellation fees. We do appreciate a heads-up if you can't make it. Repeated no-shows may result in us asking for a deposit on future bookings, but we've never had to actually do this.

We may need to relocate or reschedule a booking in rare cases (an oversight on our end, an unexpected venue issue). When this happens we'll let you know as soon as we can and offer alternatives.

Right to refuse service

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone, at any time, for any lawful reason. In practice this comes up almost never. We aim to be welcoming first.

We will refuse service to anyone visibly intoxicated or behaving in a way that makes other guests uncomfortable. This is standard for any responsible bar in Malaysia.

Age

The legal drinking age in Malaysia is 18. Both bars serve alcohol. By visiting and ordering, you confirm you're of legal drinking age. We may ask for ID if there's any doubt.

Photos and content

The photos, written content, and design of this website are owned by us. You're welcome to share links to our pages, screenshot drinks for personal use, and quote short passages with credit. Please don't republish full articles or use our images for commercial purposes without asking us first.

If we take a photo of you in the bar, we'll typically ask before posting it on our Instagram. If we miss this and you'd rather not be visible, message us and we'll take it down.

External links

This site links to external sites (Google Maps, WhatsApp, Wikipedia, font providers). We're not responsible for what happens on those sites.

Liability

We do our best to make sure both bars are safe and welcoming. By visiting, you accept that drinking alcohol carries normal risks (including but not limited to: feeling worse the next morning). Please drink responsibly. We're happy to call you a Grab.

Governing law

These terms and your use of this site are governed by Malaysian law.

Changes

If we update these terms, we'll change the date at the top. The current version always lives at dissolvedsolids.co/terms/.

Questions
