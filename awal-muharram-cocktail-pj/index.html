Awal Muharram in PJ.

A respectful, non-alcoholic-focused cocktail-bar plan for the Islamic New Year (1 Muharram) in Petaling Jaya. NA programme, quiet room, coffee and tea served with care.

Awal Muharram (the first day of Muharram) marks the Islamic New Year. It is a reflective public holiday in Malaysia, observed quietly by Muslim families through prayer, recitation, and considered new-year intentions. The cocktail bar's contribution on this day is the NA programme: serious mocktails, brewed teas, single-origin coffee, served in the same glassware as the regular menu.

The NA-focused approach

On Awal Muharram, our NA programme is the default for guests who specify they are observing. The alcoholic menu remains available for guests who are not. We pour both in matching glassware with the same care.

What to order on Awal Muharram

The drinks lean reflective and warming, not celebratory. The Islamic New Year is not a fireworks night.

Cardamom and Honey Tea Cocktail: brewed cardamom tea, honey, lemon, ice.

Rose and Saffron Cooler: rose syrup, saffron tincture, lemon, soda.

Date Old Fashioned (NA): NA whisky substitute, date syrup, aromatic bitters, large rock.

NA Bandung: rose syrup, evaporated milk, ice. Traditional Malaysian.

Pomegranate Sherbet: fresh pomegranate, lemon, sugar, soda. Light and clean.

Sirap Selasih: rose syrup with basil seeds. Quiet, traditional.

Karak Chai, Iced or Hot: proper cardamom-led milk tea.

Yemeni Coffee: single-origin Arabica with cardamom, served small.

The Awal Muharram evening plan

Daytime: family-led observance.

Evening, 8pm-10pm: a quiet sit at a small bar. One or two NA cocktails or coffees, considered conversation, no theatre.

For mixed-faith groups

The bar pours both sides in parallel. Observing guests get the NA programme; non-observing guests order the regular menu. Glassware matches.

Our two PJ outlets

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; on Awal Muharram we keep the music softer.

Soluble Solids · SS2: the smaller outlet. No printed menu. On Awal Muharram we default to the NA programme.
