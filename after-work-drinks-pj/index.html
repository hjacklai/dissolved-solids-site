After-work drinks in PJ.

A weeknight after-work cocktail plan in Petaling Jaya. Small bars that open at 5pm-6pm, the 6:30-8:30 sweet spot, low-ABV ordering, and the case for skipping the KL central commute entirely.

If your office is in PJ or the Klang Valley fringe (Cyberjaya, Subang, Damansara Heights, Bangsar South), the after-work drink does not need to happen in KL. PJ has small bars open from 5pm or 6pm, perfectly built for the "one or two cocktails before going home" weeknight format. Traffic is gentler, your home commute is shorter, and the rooms are quieter. The colleagues who live in KL central can grab a Grab; the rest of you save 30-45 minutes of evening driving.

Why PJ wins for after-work

You are not adding a KL commute. If your office is in PJ, an after-work drink in PJ means you go straight home after. The KL after-work means another 30-45 minutes of evening traffic.

The bars are quieter at 6:30-7:30pm. Most PJ craft bars do not hit capacity until 9pm. The early-evening atmosphere is calmer, the bartender has more time.

Lower prices. Same standard of drink, RM5-10 less per cocktail than central KL.

Walking distance for the locals. If colleagues live in PJ, "shall we grab one drink" becomes a walk, not a planning exercise.

The after-work format that works in PJ

  • Arrive 6:30pm-7pm. Most PJ craft bars open at 5pm or 6pm.
  • One drink minimum, two maximum. Three is a different decision; you may want to call it dinner instead.
  • Group of 3-6 colleagues is the sweet spot. Larger needs a different format.
  • Out by 8:30pm-9pm. Tomorrow is a workday.

The PJ after-work bar landscape

Small craft bars in SS2, Damansara Kim, Damansara Heights, Bangsar South: most open 5pm or 6pm, run weeknight crowds of 8-15 people through 7-9pm. Conversation-volume music. Easy to walk in and order.

Avoid: the louder rooftop programmes (those skew weekend and tourist), the cocktail-restaurants that prioritise food covers over bar covers.

What to order on a PJ after-work

Light, drinkable, lower-ABV. The reason: you are driving home and tomorrow is work.

Spritzes: Aperol, Hugo, Negroni Sbagliato. Low ABV, long pours, refreshing.

Highballs: Pandan Collins, Calamansi Highball, Whisky Highball. The drink format built for hot afternoons and easy weeknight sips.

Low-ABV vermouth drinks: Cocchi Americano with grapefruit, Bicicletta (Campari + white wine + soda), Sherry Cobbler.

If wanting a "proper cocktail": Whisky Sour, French 75. Medium ABV, drinks lighter than the spec suggests.

For colleagues who do not drink: NA spritzes (elderflower, hibiscus), kombucha-by-the-glass, our coffee mocktails (Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom).

Our two PJ outlets, for after-work

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. Opens 5pm Tue-Sun. The 6-8pm crowd is largely PJ after-work locals: media, design, food, finance. The room is warm-lit and conversation-volume.

Soluble Solids · SS2: small room on Jalan SS2/24. Wed-Sun, opens 6pm. The smaller, quieter outlet. Good for 3-4 person after-work where you want to actually hear each other.
