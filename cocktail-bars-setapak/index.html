Cocktail bar near Setapak.

From Setapak, Wangsa Maju, or Danau Kota to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 28-32 minutes by car. The closest serious cocktail bars west of the eastern-KL residential corridor.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Setapak and Wangsa Maju are dense residential blocks in the northeast of KL, with strong food culture, a large student population, and a thin cocktail-bar scene. For Setapak residents who want a serious cocktail evening, the 28 to 32 minute drive west into Petaling Jaya is the standard answer.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

The character of Setapak

Setapak runs from the Jalan Genting Klang corridor through Danau Kota, Taman Setapak, and Setapak Jaya, then bleeds north into Wangsa Maju. Two things shape the neighbourhood: a young population (TAR UMT, the colleges around Wangsa Maju, KL East students) and dense Chinese-Malaysian and Indian-Malaysian residential settlement that has been there for generations.

That mix is excellent for food. Danau Kota night-market hawker stalls, the Genting Klang kopitiams, the steamboat places around Wangsa Walk and the wider Wangsa Maju block, the Indian and banana-leaf restaurants around Setapak Central, the late-night mamak corners that fill up after 22:00. What it does not produce in any volume is the small specialist cocktail-bar format. The market in Setapak is happier with RM 20 drinks at a casual gastropub than RM 40 drinks at a six-seat back bar.

Why drive west for the drink

A serious cocktail bar trades volume for craft. Fresh-cut citrus prepped daily, a back bar of 60 or more bottles with real depth in vermouth and bitters, a bartender who can taste mid-build and adjust. PJ has owner-operated rooms of this format because rents allow it, and because the surrounding density of dinner restaurants and walkable parking sustains the foot traffic. Setapak's local cocktail venues are mostly lounge-shaped: bigger rooms, music-led, drink quality variable.

The typical Setapak move is to eat local first (steamboat or char kway teow in Wangsa Maju or Danau Kota, or a sit-down dinner around Setapak Central) and then drive to PJ for one or two properly made drinks. The 28 to 32 minute drive each way is short enough to make this work on a Thursday or Friday evening.

Drive times

  • Setapak Central: 28 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 30 to Soluble Solids.
  • Wangsa Maju: 30 minutes / 32.
  • Danau Kota: 28 minutes / 30.
  • Genting Klang: 30 minutes / 32.
  • Taman Setapak Jaya: 27 minutes / 29.
  • KL East / Melawati: 32 minutes / 35.

The cleanest route is the DUKE Expressway westbound, joining the MRR2, then exiting either at Damansara Utama (for Dissolved Solids) or Section 17 (for Soluble Solids). Off-peak the drive is mostly highway-grade with two or three sets of traffic lights at the end. Friday evening (after 18:00) the DUKE westbound is the slow stretch; budget 35 to 40 minutes instead of 28 to 30.

An alternative for the cost-conscious: from Wangsa Maju LRT (Kelana Jaya line) it is a direct ride to Taman Bahagia, then an 8 minute Grab to either bar. About 50 minutes door to door, RM 4 in fare. Useful if the driver wants to drink.

Grab fares from Setapak: RM 28 to 42 to either bar.

What to order if it is your first time

At Dissolved Solids, two house specialities to start with: the Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon, foamed) and the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan syrup, lime, soda). Both are built on Malaysian ingredients but follow international technique. If you want a classic, the Martini arrives stirred, properly cold, with a choice of olive or twist.

At Soluble Solids, do not order by name. Tell the bartender three short answers: strength (light, medium, strong), spirit preference (gin, rum, whisky, mezcal, or surprise), and flavour profile (citrusy, herbal, smoky, sweet). They build the drink in front of you from the labelled infusion shelf along the back wall.

Hours and when to come

Dissolved Solids: Tuesday to Sunday, opens 14:00 or 15:00 depending on day, closes 00:00 (Tue-Thu, Sun) or 01:00 (Fri-Sat). Closed Mondays.

Soluble Solids: Wednesday to Sunday, 18:00 to 01:00. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

For a quick after-work drink between 18:00 and 20:00, Dissolved Solids is the easier pick. For a longer settled evening with two or three drinks, Soluble Solids is the better room. Walk-ins are fine at both. WhatsApp ahead for any group of six or more, or for Friday and Saturday after 21:00.

Getting back to Setapak after

The drive back via the MRR2 and DUKE is fast post-midnight, 22 to 26 minutes with no traffic. Police checkpoints occasionally operate on the DUKE Friday and Saturday nights; never drive if you have had a drink. Grab is universal and reliable for the return at 01:00, though surge pricing applies most weekend nights (budget RM 40 to 60 back).
