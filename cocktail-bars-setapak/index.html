Cocktail bar near Setapak.

From Setapak, Wangsa Maju, or Danau Kota to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 28-32 minutes by car. The closest serious cocktail bars west of the eastern-KL residential corridor.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Setapak and Wangsa Maju are dense residential blocks in the northeast of KL, with strong food culture and a thin cocktail-bar scene. For Setapak residents who want a serious cocktail evening, the 28-32 minute drive west into PJ is the standard option.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times

  • Setapak Central: 28 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 30 to Soluble Solids.
  • Wangsa Maju: 30 minutes / 32.
  • Danau Kota: 28 minutes / 30.
  • Genting Klang: 30 minutes / 32.

Route: take the DUKE highway westbound, join the MRR2, exit at Damansara Utama or Section 17. Off-peak straight drive.

Grab fares from Setapak: RM 28-42 to either bar.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Sun, opens 14:00 or 15:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun, 18:00-01:00.
