Hari Raya Aidiladha in KL.

A respectful non-alcoholic cocktail-bar plan for Aidiladha in Kuala Lumpur. The NA programme, the coffee and tea side, and a quieter Petaling Jaya alternative for the evening.

Hari Raya Aidiladha (Feast of Sacrifice) marks the close of the Hajj. For Muslim guests across the country, the day is for prayer, qurban, and family. The bar's role on this day is the NA one: serious mocktails, brewed teas, single-origin coffee. Same craft, same glassware, no alcohol push.

The KL Aidiladha reality

  • Aidiladha is a public holiday. Most Muslim households are home with family from morning through afternoon.
  • KL central is quieter than a normal Friday or Saturday by evening.
  • Non-Muslim and mixed-faith friend groups often gather at neutral venues from late afternoon into evening.
  • A craft bar with a real NA programme is the right kind of neutral venue.

What to order on Aidiladha at a KL bar

Drinks drawing on Malaysian and Middle Eastern flavours, both reflecting the day.

Rose and Cardamom Cooler: rose syrup, cardamom, lime, soda.

Date and Tamarind Sour: date syrup, tamarind, lemon, ice.

Saffron and Honey Lemonade: saffron-infused syrup, honey, lemon, soda.

NA Bandung: rose syrup, evaporated milk, ice. Malaysian classic in a wine glass.

Sirap Selasih: rose syrup with basil seeds.

Pandan and Coconut Refresher: pandan syrup, coconut water, lime, soda.

Iced Karak Chai: proper karak chai with cardamom and saffron.

Kopi-O Peng in a Coupe: cold-brewed kopi-O with gula melaka.

Coffee and tea on Aidiladha

  • Cold-brewed kopi-O with gula melaka, in a coupe
  • Karak chai (cardamom-led milk tea), iced or hot
  • Yemeni-style coffee with cardamom
  • Single-origin pour-overs
  • Brewed jasmine tea, hot or iced

The Petaling Jaya alternative

If you want a quieter, more contemplative room for the Aidiladha evening, PJ is 15-25 minutes by Grab.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; on Aidiladha we keep music softer than usual.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, no printed menu. On Aidiladha we run the NA programme as default.

For mixed-faith groups

The bar handles both sides. The bartender pours the NA cocktail for observing guests and the alcoholic version for others, in matching glassware, at the same time. No announcement, no awkwardness.

The Aidiladha evening plan

Daytime: prayers, qurban, family lunch.

Afternoon visits: open-house rounds.

Evening, 8pm-10pm: a quiet sit at the bar. NA cocktails, coffee, tea.
