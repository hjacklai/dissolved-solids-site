Cocktail bar near Shah Alam.

Shah Alam itself has very few licensed cocktail venues by local convention. The closest serious cocktail bars are 25-30 minutes east in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Shah Alam is the Selangor state capital and a large residential township. By long-standing local convention, the city has a thin licensed-venue scene; cocktail bars are nearly nonexistent inside Shah Alam proper. For a cocktail evening, the 25-30 minute drive east into Petaling Jaya is the standard answer.

Our two bars

Dissolved Solids, Damansara Kim, PJ. 43-1 Jalan SS20/11. Maps · WhatsApp

Soluble Solids, SS2, PJ. 50-1 Jalan SS2/24. Maps · WhatsApp

Drive times from Shah Alam areas

  • Shah Alam city centre / Stadium: 28 minutes to Dissolved Solids, 30 to Soluble Solids.
  • Setia Alam: 30 minutes / 33.
  • Bukit Jelutong: 22 minutes / 25.
  • Kota Kemuning: 30 minutes / 32.
  • Subang Bestari: 25 minutes / 28.
  • Alam Avenue / Setia City: 25 minutes / 28.

Route: Federal Highway eastbound, exit at Damansara Utama for Dissolved Solids, continue to Section 17 for Soluble Solids. Off-peak straight drive.

Grab fares from Shah Alam: RM 28-42 to either bar.

Hours and reservations

Dissolved Solids: Tue-Sun, opens 14:00 or 15:00.

Soluble Solids: Wed-Sun, 18:00-01:00.

WhatsApp ahead if you are a group of 6+ or arriving late on a Saturday.

Note on going back

If you are driving home to Shah Alam after drinks: please plan ahead. Grab is the right call. Both bars will happily call you a Grab when you are ready to leave, and the wait time from PJ to Shah Alam is usually 5-8 minutes off-peak.
