Cocktail bar near Shah Alam.

Shah Alam itself has very few licensed cocktail venues by local convention. The closest serious cocktail bars are 25-30 minutes east in Petaling Jaya: Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2.

Updated May 2026·Petaling Jaya

Shah Alam is the Selangor state capital and one of the largest planned cities in the country, organised into numbered sections that fan out from the Sultan Salahuddin Abdul Aziz Mosque. The licensed-venue scene inside Shah Alam proper is thin by long-standing local convention; dedicated cocktail bars are rare. For a cocktail evening, the 22 to 30 minute drive east into Petaling Jaya is the standard answer for most Shah Alam residents.

The character of Shah Alam

Shah Alam stretches from the Federal Highway corridor in the north to Kota Kemuning in the south, with UiTM Shah Alam (one of the country's largest universities) anchoring Section 7 and the Blue Mosque marking the city's symbolic centre. The older sections (1 through 17) are mostly residential-with-pockets-of-commercial. The newer suburbs to the west (Setia Alam, Bukit Jelutong, Kota Kemuning) are mid-to-upper-tier gated developments. I-City and Setia City Mall are the largest F and B nodes inside the wider Shah Alam area.

The drinking culture in Shah Alam proper is structurally lighter than in PJ or central KL. There are some licensed restaurants inside hotels and a handful of casual venues, but the small specialist cocktail-bar format (a 50-seat room with a 60-bottle back bar, fresh-cut citrus, customised drinks) does not have a foothold inside the city. Setia Alam and Bukit Jelutong have a few more options because their gated-community demographics support more F and B, but even there the format is mostly restaurant-bar rather than dedicated cocktail bar.

Why drive east for the drink

The PJ corridor sits directly east of Shah Alam along the Federal Highway. SS2, Damansara Kim, and Section 17 are between 22 and 30 minutes from most Shah Alam addresses. That puts a serious cocktail bar within the same drive time as the I-City Mall or the Sunway Pyramid, so for a Shah Alam-resident evening it pencils out as a reasonable trade.

The other consideration is parking. Both our PJ outlets have free street parking and a covered municipal lot within a three-minute walk, with no reservation needed. That removes one of the more common Shah Alam-to-KL pain points, where parking near a comparable cocktail venue in KLCC can cost RM 15 to 20 and take 10 minutes to find.

Friday peak (17:30 to 19:00) eastbound on the Federal Highway adds 10 to 15 minutes.

What to order if it is your first time

At Dissolved Solids, two house specialities: the Kopi Sour (whisky, kopi-O, gula melaka, lemon, foamed) and the Pandan Collins (gin, fresh pandan, lime, soda). Both are built around Malaysian ingredients using classical international technique. If you would prefer something more familiar, the Martini arrives properly stirred, properly cold.

At Soluble Solids, do not order by name. Tell the bartender three short answers: strength (light, medium, strong), spirit (gin, rum, whisky, mezcal, surprise), flavour direction (citrusy, herbal, smoky, sweet). The drink is built in front of you from the wall of labelled infusion jars.

Both bars carry a real non-alcoholic menu (not just sweet mocktails). If you are joining a Shah Alam group that includes non-drinkers, this matters; see our guide for non-drinkers.

Hours and when to come

Dissolved Solids: Tuesday to Sunday, opens 14:00 or 15:00 depending on day, closes 00:00 (Tue-Thu, Sun) or 01:00 (Fri-Sat). Closed Mondays.

Soluble Solids: Wednesday to Sunday, 18:00 to 01:00. Closed Mondays and Tuesdays.

For a post-work drink at 19:00 to 20:00, Dissolved Solids is the easier pick because it opens earliest and is usually less full at that hour. For a longer settled evening, Soluble Solids reads better. Walk-ins are welcome at both. WhatsApp ahead for groups of six or more or for Friday/Saturday after 21:00.

Getting back to Shah Alam after

If you are driving home: please plan ahead. The Federal Highway westbound has occasional police checkpoints on Friday and Saturday nights, and the penalties for drink-driving in Malaysia are strict. The safe move is Grab home. Both bars will call a Grab for you when you are ready to leave; pickup wait from PJ to Shah Alam is usually 5 to 8 minutes off-peak.

Grab fares back to Shah Alam: RM 28 to 50 depending on time of day and surge. The Federal Highway westbound is generally quiet after 23:00, so the trip home is fast.
