Maulidur Rasul in PJ.

A respectful non-alcoholic cocktail-bar plan for Maulidur Rasul (Prophet Muhammad's birthday) in Petaling Jaya. NA programme, coffee and tea, considered presentation.

Maulidur Rasul commemorates the birthday of Prophet Muhammad. In Malaysia it is a public holiday observed across mosques and Muslim households with marhaban recitations, parades, and gatherings. The cocktail bar's role on this day is the NA programme, served quietly and respectfully.

The NA-focused approach

On Maulidur Rasul, our NA programme is the default for observing guests. Same craft, same glassware, same care as the regular menu. The alcoholic side remains available for guests who are not observing.

What to order on Maulidur Rasul

The drinks lean warm-spiced and floral, reflecting the day's reflective tone.

Rose Sherbet: rose syrup, lemon, soda, dried rose petal garnish. Floral and gentle.

Cardamom and Date Cooler: brewed cardamom tea, date syrup, lemon, ice. Sweet, warm, contemplative.

Saffron Honey Lemonade: saffron-infused syrup, honey, lemon, soda.

NA Bandung: rose syrup, evaporated milk, ice. Malaysian classic.

Pomegranate and Rose Spritz: fresh pomegranate, rose syrup, soda.

Sirap Selasih: rose syrup with basil seeds.

Karak Chai: proper cardamom-led milk tea, iced or hot.

Yemeni Coffee: single-origin Arabica with cardamom.

The Maulidur Rasul evening plan

Daytime: marhaban, prayer, family gatherings.

Evening, 8pm-10pm: quiet sit at a small bar. One or two NA cocktails or coffees, no theatre.

Our two PJ outlets

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Quiet by default; on Maulidur Rasul we keep the music softer.

Soluble Solids · SS2: the smaller outlet. NA programme as default on Maulidur Rasul.
