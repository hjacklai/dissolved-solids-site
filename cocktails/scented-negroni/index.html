The Scented Negroni.

One of our house signatures. Equal-parts Negroni layered with bruised kaffir lime leaf and fresh dark chocolate shavings. Smells like a garden, drinks like a proper aperitif, finishes like cocoa. Built to evolve as it dilutes.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim·House creation

Ingredients

  • London Dry gin
  • Campari
  • Sweet vermouth
  • 1 fresh kaffir lime leaf, bruised
  • A few small dark chocolate shavings, on top
  • Large ice cube to serve

Method

  1. Bruise the kaffir lime leaf between your palms to wake the oils. Drop it into a chilled mixing glass.
  2. Add gin, Campari, and vermouth. Add ice.
  3. Stir long, about 25 to 30 seconds. Dilution matters.
  4. Strain over a large ice cube in a rocks glass.
  5. Shave dark chocolate on top so the cocoa oils rise as the drink warms.

Measurements are a guide. Our bartenders adjust ratios at service for the best balance. Natural ingredients (citrus, herbs, syrups, fresh fruit) shift in intensity through the season.

How it tastes

Classic Negroni bitterness up front, then the kaffir lime leaf hits in the nose as you bring the glass closer. The dark chocolate doesn't sweeten the drink. It softens the bitter edge and adds a low cocoa register that pulls everything together. The drink opens up as the ice melts. We've found the most interesting sip is the third or fourth, when dilution has done its work.

Why we built it

The Negroni is one of those drinks that resists reinvention. Anything you add tends to muddy the original equation. The brief here was to find aromatics that play with the existing bitter-sweet base, not against it. Kaffir lime leaf turned out to be a near-perfect match for Campari, somehow making the bitter feel brighter without changing the body. Dark chocolate was the part that took longer. We tried cocoa powder, chocolate bitters, and a chocolate infusion. Shavings on top, fresh, won.

The signature framing

The Scented Negroni sits alongside the Tesseract as one of our two house signatures. Where the Tesseract is built around four flavour dimensions interlocking geometrically, the Scented Negroni works in two layers. The classic Negroni structure underneath; the Malaysian-coded aromatic (kaffir lime leaf) and the rounding dark-chocolate finish on top. Two simple modifications, big change in character.

It is the Negroni we hand to guests who tell us they like Negronis but want to see a different version. The base ratio is canonical (1:1:1, gin to Campari to sweet vermouth). The kaffir lime is the Klang Valley signature; the chocolate is the modern bartender's softener for bitter drinks.
