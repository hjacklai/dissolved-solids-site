Black Honey

A non-alcoholic coffee mocktail with pink grapefruit, smoked herbs, nectarivore honey, mango, and espresso. Bitter, smoky, faintly tropical. Zero alcohol.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • 30ml fresh espresso, chilled
  • 30ml fresh pink grapefruit juice
  • 15ml mango puree
  • 15ml nectarivore honey syrup
  • Soda or chilled filtered water to top
  • Smoked rosemary or thyme sprig
  • (zero alcohol)

Method

  1. Smoke the herb sprig over a flame for 5 seconds, set aside.
  2. Combine espresso, grapefruit juice, mango, and honey in a shaker over plenty of ice.
  3. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds.
  4. Double-strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.
  5. Top lightly with soda for length.
  6. Garnish so the smoke rises into the nose.

How it tastes

Bitter espresso up front, the grapefruit cuts in mid-palate, then the honey and mango wrap the finish in something almost floral. The smoked herb is the part that surprises people. It hits before the first sip, then keeps showing up in the nose between sips. We've found this is a good gateway for people who don't usually order non-alcoholic drinks.

Why we built it

This sits in our Crafted Beverages section, which is the non-alcoholic side of the menu. We've come to think a good mocktail should not feel like a cocktail with the alcohol subtracted. Black Honey was an attempt at a savoury, complex pour that earns its place on a menu without any spirits at all. Pink grapefruit was the unlock. It bridges the bitter coffee and the soft honey without either dominating. More on our mocktail philosophy here.
