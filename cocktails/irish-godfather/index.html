Irish Godfather

Whisky, amaretto, coffee liqueur, espresso. The Godfather, slowed down by espresso.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • Irish whiskey (or a soft, lightly peated whisky)
  • Amaretto
  • Coffee liqueur
  • Fresh espresso, chilled
  • Large ice cube
  • Coffee bean to float

Method

  1. Combine everything in a shaker over plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds so the crema lands clean.
  3. Strain over a large ice cube in a rocks glass.
  4. Float a coffee bean on the surface.

Measurements are a guide. Our bartenders adjust ratios at service for the best balance. Natural ingredients (citrus, herbs, syrups, fresh fruit) shift in intensity through the season.

How it tastes

Whisky and amaretto give the warm body of a classic Godfather. The espresso adds bitterness and weight; the coffee liqueur extends the espresso's sweetness without sugar. Drinks slow. Ages well in the glass. We've had people order this and stay an extra hour without ordering anything else.

Why we built it

The Godfather (whisky and amaretto) is one of those classics that benefits from gentle modernisation. Espresso was the easiest, most coherent addition. The drink stays in the same family but gains depth.

Where to drink it

Frequently asked questions

What glass is the Irish Godfather served in?

A rocks glass with one large ice cube. Short and heavy. The single rock holds the cold while the drink develops over 15 to 20 minutes, and the espresso's crema sits on the surface where the nose can find it. A coupe loses the big-rock pacing that this drink is built around.

Can I substitute the Irish whiskey?

Yes. Any soft, lightly peated or unpeated whisky works. A blended Scotch (Famous Grouse, Monkey Shoulder) is the common swap. Bourbon gives a sweeter, vanilla-led version. A heavy peated Islay like Laphroaig overwhelms the espresso. Japanese blended whiskies sit well with the coffee and the amaretto. The key is gentle, not aggressive.

How strong is the Irish Godfather?

Around 22 to 26 percent ABV in the glass after shaker dilution and the big-rock melt. The espresso adds caffeine but also volume, which lengthens the drink without dropping its strength too far. Built as an after-dinner pour. One is satisfying; two and you'll be wide awake at midnight.

Also on request at Soluble Solids in SS2, Petaling Jaya (50-1 Jalan SS2/24, Wed to Sun 18:00 to 01:00, WhatsApp +60 11-1682 8651). Both bars are in Tatler Asia Top 20 Bars 2025/26. Best ordered after dinner; the espresso is pulled fresh for the drink.

What food pairs with the Irish Godfather?

Dark chocolate at 70 percent or above is the obvious pairing. Tiramisu, affogato, panna cotta. Salted caramel desserts. A cheese plate with aged comté or a runny taleggio. For Malaysian context, kuih lapis with its layered sweetness or a slice of pandan chiffon cake. Avoid anything fresh or citrus-led; the drink wants depth, not lift.
