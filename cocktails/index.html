The Full List · Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Everything we pour.

The whole list, organised the way we'd order it ourselves. Malaysian local for the home-pantry classics. Stirred for slow nights. Shaken when you want texture. Built for long sits. Vermouth-based if you're trying to keep it light. Coffee cocktails for the in-between hours. Crafted beverages for when you're staying off the alcohol. Seasonal for what we're playing with this month. Some have their own page; click in to read about them.
