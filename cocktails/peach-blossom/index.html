Peach Blossom

Peach, sakura, earl grey, and espresso. One of our coffee-led low-ABV pours. Drinks like dessert, weighs like a snack.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • 30ml espresso liqueur
  • 20ml fresh espresso, chilled
  • 20ml peach puree
  • 10ml sakura syrup
  • 15ml cold-brewed earl grey tea
  • Dehydrated peach slice to garnish

Method

  1. Combine espresso liqueur, espresso, peach puree, sakura syrup, and cold-brewed earl grey in a shaker.
  2. Shake hard with ice for 12 to 15 seconds. The earl grey helps hold the foam.
  3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Slip a dehydrated peach slice on the rim.

How it tastes

Peach up front, espresso in the body, earl grey on the long finish. The bergamot in the earl grey is the most important ingredient even though it's the least obvious. Without it, the drink reads as a peach espresso martini. With it, the drink reads as something more complicated, with a tea-leaf register that pulls everything together. The sakura is subtle and shows up mostly in the nose.

Why we built it

The low-ABV section of our menu exists because we drink ourselves and we don't always want a strong cocktail. The pours in this section sit around 8 to 12 percent ABV (about half a regular cocktail). That budget makes you think differently about flavour: you can't lean on alcohol to carry the drink, so the ingredients have to do more work. Peach Blossom was an attempt at making something that drank like a coffee dessert without leaving you flattened.
