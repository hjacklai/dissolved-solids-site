Floral Mango

A non-alcoholic coffee mocktail. Apple flower tea, mango, osmanthus, espresso. The soft one in the Crafted Beverages section.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • Fresh espresso, chilled
  • Mango puree
  • Apple flower tea, cold-brewed strong
  • Osmanthus syrup
  • Soda or chilled filtered water to top
  • Edible flower or osmanthus dust to finish
  • (zero alcohol)

Method

  1. Combine espresso, mango, apple flower tea, and osmanthus in a shaker with plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds.
  3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.
  4. Top lightly with soda for length.
  5. Float an edible flower or dust the foam with osmanthus.

Measurements are a guide. Our bartenders adjust ratios at service for the best balance. Natural ingredients (citrus, herbs, syrups, fresh fruit) shift in intensity through the season.

How it tastes

Mango leads. Apple flower and osmanthus sit underneath as a soft floral platform. The espresso reads as depth rather than bitterness in this context, because the mango softens it and the floral notes layer on top. We've found this is one of the easiest gateway drinks for friends who don't usually order non-alcoholic drinks at a bar.

Why we built it

This is in our Crafted Beverages section, which is the non-alcoholic side of the menu. Mango on its own is too sweet for a sit-down beverage. Pairing it with floral notes (apple flower, osmanthus) gives it structure. Espresso is the part that earns the drink its place. Without coffee, this would just be a fruit drink.
