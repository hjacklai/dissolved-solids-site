Floral Mango

Apple flower tea, mango, osmanthus, espresso. The soft one in the coffee section.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • 30ml espresso liqueur
  • 20ml fresh espresso, chilled
  • 20ml mango puree
  • 15ml apple flower tea, cold-brewed strong
  • 10ml osmanthus syrup
  • Edible flower or osmanthus dust to finish

Method

  1. Combine all liquids in a shaker with plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds.
  3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Float an edible flower or dust the foam with osmanthus.

How it tastes

Mango leads. Apple flower and osmanthus sit underneath as a soft floral platform. The espresso reads as depth rather than bitterness in this context, because the mango softens it and the floral notes layer on top. We've found this is one of the easiest gateway drinks for friends who think coffee cocktails are too intense.

Why we built it

Mango on its own is too sweet for a cocktail. Pairing it with floral notes (apple flower, osmanthus) gives it structure. Espresso is the part that earns the drink its place in the Crafted Beverage section. Without coffee, this would just be a fruit drink.

Where to drink it
