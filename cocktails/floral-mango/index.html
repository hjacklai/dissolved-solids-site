Floral Mango

A non-alcoholic coffee mocktail. Apple flower tea, mango, osmanthus, espresso. The soft one in the Crafted Beverages section.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • Fresh espresso, chilled
  • Mango puree
  • Apple flower tea, cold-brewed strong
  • Osmanthus syrup
  • Soda or chilled filtered water to top
  • Edible flower or osmanthus dust to finish
  • (zero alcohol)

Method

  1. Combine espresso, mango, apple flower tea, and osmanthus in a shaker with plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds.
  3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.
  4. Top lightly with soda for length.
  5. Float an edible flower or dust the foam with osmanthus.

Measurements are a guide. Our bartenders adjust ratios at service for the best balance. Natural ingredients (citrus, herbs, syrups, fresh fruit) shift in intensity through the season.

How it tastes

Mango leads. Apple flower and osmanthus sit underneath as a soft floral platform. The espresso reads as depth rather than bitterness in this context, because the mango softens it and the floral notes layer on top. We've found this is one of the easiest gateway drinks for friends who don't usually order non-alcoholic drinks at a bar.

Why we built it

This is in our Crafted Beverages section, which is the non-alcoholic side of the menu. Mango on its own is too sweet for a sit-down beverage. Pairing it with floral notes (apple flower, osmanthus) gives it structure. Espresso is the part that earns the drink its place. Without coffee, this would just be a fruit drink. Frequently asked questions

What glass is the Floral Mango served in?

A chilled coupe or short rocks glass, double-strained, with an edible flower floating or osmanthus dust dusted across the foam. Coupe is the photogenic choice. A rocks glass works if you want more length from a soda top. Highball loses the foam texture, which is part of the visual signature.

Can I substitute the apple flower tea?

Chamomile tea is the closest substitute, gentle and slightly apple-honeyed. White tea works for a more delicate base. Jasmine tea pushes the drink too floral and clashes with the osmanthus. Avoid black tea; the tannin punches up the bitterness and ruins the soft balance. Apple flower is the unlock; chamomile is the practical fallback.

How strong is the Floral Mango?

Zero alcohol. The drink contains no spirit. Caffeine is the only stimulant (about 60 to 80mg from the espresso, plus traces from the apple flower tea). Safe to drive after; not ideal right before bed. The fruit-and-floral profile makes it readable to people who do not normally order mocktails.

What food pairs with the Floral Mango?

Brunch and Asian sweets. Avocado toast, salmon bagel, dim sum, kueh, mango sticky rice, coconut pudding. Also works as a stand-alone afternoon drink. The mango-and-floral profile bridges into white-fish ceviche or scallop crudo. Avoid spicy heat; the gentle floral notes get crushed by chilli.
