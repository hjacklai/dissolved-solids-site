Pyrus Gimlet

A gimlet built around fresh pear, gin, lime, and a touch of elderflower. The friendliest gin drink on our list.

Served at Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim

Ingredients

  • London Dry gin
  • Fresh pear puree
  • Fresh lime juice
  • Elderflower liqueur or cordial
  • Sugar syrup, to taste
  • Pear slice or lime cheek to garnish

Method

  1. Combine gin, pear, lime, elderflower, and syrup over plenty of ice.
  2. Shake hard for 12 to 15 seconds.
  3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a thin pear slice or an expressed lime cheek.

Measurements are a guide. Our bartenders adjust ratios at service for the best balance. Natural ingredients (citrus, herbs, syrups, fresh fruit) shift in intensity through the season.

How it tastes

Pear softens the gin and lets the juniper sit gently underneath. Lime keeps everything bright. Elderflower adds the floral lift on the finish. Drinks dry, not sweet, despite the fruit. We've found this is one of the easiest crowd-pleasers when someone says "I don't usually like gin".

Why we built it

The classic gimlet is gin and lime cordial. Beautiful but unforgiving. Adding fresh pear gives the drink rounded body without losing the gimlet shape. Elderflower is the small bridge between the pear and the gin's botanicals. The whole drink reads as a more grown-up version of a fruit-forward cocktail.

Where to drink it
