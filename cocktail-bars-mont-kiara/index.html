Cocktail bar near Mont Kiara.

From Mont Kiara to either of our two PJ cocktail bars: 14 to 17 minutes by car. Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim, Soluble Solids in SS2. Both are worth the short drive.

Updated May 2026·Mont Kiara, KL

Mont Kiara has plenty of bars and rooftops, but most of them lean into the loud expat-rooftop format. If you are looking for a quieter, more drink-focused room, a 14 to 17 minute drive west takes you to Dissolved Solids in Damansara Kim or Soluble Solids in SS2. Both sit firmly in the cocktail-bar category rather than the lounge-club category, and both have made the short trip from Mont Kiara a regular routine for many of our guests.

The drive

Both routes are short. From Plaza Mont Kiara, off Jalan Kiara, the drive is:

  • To Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim): 14 minutes off-peak. Drive south through Sri Hartamas, cross the LDP at Damansara Heights, continue along Lebuhraya Damansara-Puchong to the Damansara Utama exit, two turns into SS20. 17 minutes during Friday peak.
  • To Soluble Solids (SS2): 17 minutes off-peak. Similar route, continue further south after the LDP exit through Section 17, then into the SS2 grid. 20 minutes during Friday peak.

By Grab: RM 18-25 to either bar.

Why Mont Kiara residents come west

The honest reasons we hear:

1. The cocktail itself. A serious cocktail bar invests in spirit selection, glassware, ice quality, and bartender training in a way that a lounge with a big drinks menu cannot. We pour the kind of drinks that reward your palate getting better over time.

2. Quiet enough to talk. Both rooms seat around 30 people. The music sits under conversation. You can hear what the person across the table is saying without leaning in.

3. Parking is genuinely easier. Both bars have free street parking after 18:00, with covered municipal lots within a 3-minute walk. Mont Kiara parking is RM 5+/hour at most rooftop venues.

Which one to pick

Pick Dissolved Solids (Damansara Kim) if you want a printed cocktail list, are coming early (the bar opens at 14:00 or 15:00), want food on the table, want to try coffee cocktails done well.

Pick Soluble Solids (SS2) if you want a drink built specifically for you, are coming later (the bar opens at 18:00), prefer the smaller-and-quieter room, want to spend time talking to the bartender.
