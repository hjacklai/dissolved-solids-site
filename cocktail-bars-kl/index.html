Cocktail bars in KL.

Both of our cocktail bars sit just outside KL city centre, in Petaling Jaya. From KLCC, Bukit Bintang, Bangsar, or Mont Kiara, the drive is 15 to 25 minutes off-peak. Quieter rooms, easier parking, the same quality of drinks.

Updated May 2026·Tatler Asia Top 20 Bars 2025/26

KL proper has plenty of cocktail bars. Many of them are excellent. What KL has less of, at any price point, is the small, quiet, owner-operated cocktail room, the kind of place built for talking and slow drinking rather than for the loud rooftop or club-adjacent format. The PJ corridor west of KL has more of those, and our two outlets are part of that scene.

Why drive west out of KL for a cocktail

Three structural advantages PJ has over KL:

Parking. PJ commercial zones have free street parking after 18:00. KLCC parking is RM 5-8/hour at most rooftop venues, plus a multi-storey carpark to navigate at midnight.

Volume. KL cocktail bars tend to lean into the loud, lounge-shaped format. PJ has more small rooms (30-50 seats) where conversation works.

Bartender focus. Lower rent in PJ has let many small operators invest more in spirit selection and training rather than in interiors. The cocktails are reliably more interesting per ringgit.

If you do prefer to drink in KL itself

KL has plenty of good cocktail bars. We are not the only option, and we are not claiming to be the best across the whole metro area. We are claiming that for many KL residents, the short drive west is worth it more often than they assume. Try it once.
