Private event cocktail bar in KL.

Renting a cocktail bar in Kuala Lumpur for a private event. How buyouts work, what they cost, what they include, how to brief the bar, and a Petaling Jaya alternative for smaller, more intimate buyouts.

A private event at a cocktail bar is a useful answer when the formats above (group hangout, birthday, anniversary) become too big for the regular reservation flow. You have 20+ guests, you want the room to yourselves, and you want the bar to make drinks at the standard you would order off the regular menu. Both KL and PJ have small bars that handle this. Here is the format.

When a private event makes sense

You probably want a full or partial bar buyout when:

  • Group size is 15-50 people.
  • You want the room private (no other customers seated nearby).
  • You want a custom menu (a tailored cocktail list, custom drink for the host, branded drinks).
  • The event has a fixed timing (e.g. "8pm to 11pm, exclusive use").
  • Corporate sponsors, brand activations, product launches.
  • A wedding rehearsal dinner, a "thank you to the team" night, a milestone birthday.

What a buyout typically includes

For our PJ outlets (we cover the standard format; we cannot speak for other KL bars, but the structure is similar):

  • Exclusive use of the room for the agreed window (typically 4-5 hours).
  • The full cocktail programme available, plus any custom drinks briefed in advance.
  • The bartender(s) dedicated to your group.
  • The music programming coordinated to the event (we can run our usual or fit a specific brief).
  • Bar snacks at the standard menu or, for an additional charge, a catered food spread.
  • Branded materials on request (custom napkins, recipe cards for the night, a small printed cocktail menu).

What a buyout typically costs

The standard structure is a minimum spend, not a flat rental fee. We hold the room for you against a minimum that reflects the regular revenue we would expect for that night. If the guests order enough drinks to hit the minimum, no further fee. If not, the difference is charged.

Minimums vary by night (weekends cost more), capacity, and the catering involved. WhatsApp for a quote with the date, group size, and event description.

The KL private-event landscape

KL has several bars that handle 30-100 person buyouts: rooftop bars, hotel bars, the mid-sized cocktail-bar-restaurants. The trade-off is between size and intimacy. The bigger the bar, the larger the buyout has to be to justify the rental.

Small craft bars (20-40 seats) handle 20-30 person buyouts well. The room feels full, the bartender can dedicate attention, and the drink standard does not drop.

How to brief us

For a private event, WhatsApp with:

  • Date, start time, expected end time.
  • Group size (and likely range).
  • Event type (birthday, wedding rehearsal, corporate, friends-only, etc.).
  • Whether food is needed (we can do canapes or bar snacks; full dinner needs catering).
  • Whether you want a custom menu or branded drinks.
  • Music preferences (we run a default playlist; can adjust).
  • Any "must-have" guest names, allergies, non-drinkers.
  • Budget range or whether you want us to quote against the minimum spend.

We will respond within 24 hours with a proposed format, the minimum spend, and confirmation of availability.

The custom drink menu

For private events we can build a 4-6 drink tailored menu for the night. The brief: the kind of evening, any flavours or ingredients you love, anything you want to avoid, and any "signature" drink for the host or honouree.

The custom menu is printed in small recipe-card format, named for the event (e.g. "Anna and Jay's drinks for 14 May"), and the bartender opens the night by presenting it to your guests.

This costs nothing extra (it is part of how we work) but it does require 2-3 weeks lead time so we can build and trial the drinks.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

For smaller, more intimate buyouts (15-30 people), PJ is excellent. The rooms are smaller, the prices lower, and you get the same standard of craft cocktail.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar above MyNews on Jalan SS20/11. Capacity 30 seated, up to 40 with standing. Best for buyouts of 18-35 guests.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, capacity 20 seated, up to 25 with standing. Best for buyouts of 12-22 guests. The bespoke-build format suits intimate events particularly well.

For both outlets, full-room buyouts run Tue-Thu typically and Fri-Sat with higher minimum spend.
