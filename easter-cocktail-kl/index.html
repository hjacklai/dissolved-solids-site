Easter weekend in KL.

An Easter weekend cocktail plan for Kuala Lumpur. Spring-coded drinks, Sunday brunch and roast pairings, NA programme, and a quieter Petaling Jaya alternative.

Easter is a working-week weekend in West Malaysia (Good Friday is a public holiday in Sabah and Sarawak only). For Christian families across the Klang Valley, the weekend still gathers everyone: a Good Friday service, a quiet Saturday, an Easter Sunday roast or brunch, an Easter Monday wind-down. At a Kuala Lumpur craft cocktail bar, the weekend rewards spring-coded drinks and a flexible NA programme.

The KL Easter weekend

  • Hotel brunch programmes run Easter Sunday specials (eggs, ham, chocolate fountains). Book 2-3 weeks ahead.
  • Independent cocktail bars run normal hours; some open earlier on Easter Sunday for the brunch crowd.
  • The city is moderate-traffic. The big tourist crowds are not in town for Easter.

What to order on Easter weekend in KL

French 75: gin, lemon, sugar, champagne. The Easter brunch classic.

Bellini: peach puree, prosecco.

Mimosa: orange juice, prosecco. The brunch default.

Aviation: gin, lemon, maraschino, creme de violette.

Hugo Spritz: elderflower, prosecco, mint, soda.

Chocolate Espresso Martini: vodka, kahlua, espresso, dark chocolate. The Easter egg.

Hazelnut Old Fashioned: bourbon, hazelnut syrup, aromatic bitters.

Cucumber Gimlet: gin, fresh cucumber, lime.

Sunday brunch and roast pairings

Brunch: Mimosa, Bellini, French 75. Long pours with eggs, ham, hot cross buns.

Roast: the protein dictates. Lamb pairs with Negroni or red wine. Ham pairs with Hugo Spritz or Whisky Sour. Salmon pairs with champagne or Aviation.

Dessert: Chocolate Espresso Martini, Hazelnut Old Fashioned, vintage port.

The Petaling Jaya alternative

For a quieter Easter Sunday than a central-KL hotel brunch, PJ is 15-25 minutes south.

Dissolved Solids · Damansara Kim: small upstairs bar on Jalan SS20/11. Brunch hours can be arranged for Easter Sunday group bookings.

Soluble Solids · SS2: smaller, no printed menu. Tell the bartender it is Easter.

For non-drinking guests

  • NA Mimosa: OJ + NA sparkling.
  • Elderflower spritz NA: elderflower cordial, lemon, soda.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • NA Bellini: peach + NA sparkling.
