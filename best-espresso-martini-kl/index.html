Best Espresso Martini in KL.

Where to find the best Espresso Martini in Kuala Lumpur and Petaling Jaya. What makes a proper one (fresh espresso, real foam, the right ratio), why bartenders disagree about the recipe, and our take.

The Espresso Martini has been the most-ordered cocktail in Kuala Lumpur for about three years running. It is the highest-leverage benchmark drink for any cocktail bar: fresh ingredients, technical foam, ratio precision, all within a 90-second build. A bad Espresso Martini reveals a lot about a bar. Here is what to look for and where to find the working ones.

What makes a proper Espresso Martini

The drink is structurally simple: vodka, fresh espresso, coffee liqueur, sugar. Four ingredients. But the proper build requires four things most bars get wrong:

1. The espresso must be hot AND fresh. Pulled within 60 seconds of the shake. Cold-stored espresso loses its crema entirely; pre-pulled espresso loses 50% of the aromatic oils within minutes.

2. Shake hard, fast, COLD. 12-15 seconds, full speed, ice-cold tin. The shake aerates the espresso oils into the foam. Half-hearted shaking produces a sad flat top.

3. Coffee liqueur, not coffee syrup. Mr Black Cold Brew Liqueur, Kahlúa Espresso, Tia Maria. Not coffee-flavoured syrup, not espresso powder, not coffee concentrate.

4. The foam is everything. A proper Espresso Martini should land with a 5-8mm crema-coloured foam top that holds for the first 2-3 minutes. Three coffee beans floated dead-centre on the foam (some bars use cocoa nibs, equally good).

The KL Espresso Martini landscape

Hotel bars: Bar Trigona at Four Seasons, Heli Lounge Bar at Menara KH, and other higher-end hotel bars usually pour a competent Espresso Martini. Expect RM 50-70 per drink. Quality is consistent because the espresso machines are commercial-grade.

Asia's 50 Best Bars Malaysia (Penrose, Three X Co., Bar Trigona, Reka): all four serve Espresso Martinis but typically in evolved or signature forms (Penrose has a "Brown Butter Espresso Martini" variant; Three X Co. uses a house Cold Brew base). RM 55-80 per drink.

Mid-range craft cocktail bars in KL and PJ: the working zone. Most pour Espresso Martinis competently at RM 38-50.

Restaurant bars: variable. Better at restaurants with serious espresso programmes (Stüdyo, Coppersmith, Yellow Brick Road). Risky at restaurants where the espresso machine is "for desserts only".

Mall bars: mostly skip. Pre-batched syrup-based Espresso Martinis are common at chain mall bars and rarely live up to the canonical drink.

Our Espresso Martini

We pour Espresso Martinis at both Dissolved Solids and Soluble Solids. Our build:

  • Vodka (Belvedere or Absolut Elyx) 50ml
  • Fresh-pulled espresso (single shot, 30ml, made-to-order) 30ml
  • Coffee liqueur (Mr Black or Kahlúa) 15ml
  • Demerara syrup 5-10ml (we adjust based on espresso bitterness)
  • Hard shake, 12 seconds, very cold tin
  • Double-strain into a chilled coupe
  • Garnish: three coffee beans

The Malaysian twist: our Kopi Sour variant

Malaysian local-coffee tradition produces stronger, more chocolate-heavy coffee than Italian espresso. We pour a Kopi Sour that takes the Espresso Martini structure and substitutes cold-brewed kopi-O for espresso, plus gula melaka for demerara, plus whisky for vodka. The result drinks differently from a classic Espresso Martini, fuller-bodied and more roasted-coffee-forward.

Variations to know

Vegan Espresso Martini: swap demerara syrup for agave or maple. The cleanest substitution; nobody notices the difference.

Mezcal Espresso Martini: swap half the vodka for mezcal. The smoke layered with coffee produces a distinct flavour. Cult favourite.

Brown Butter Espresso Martini: fat-wash the vodka with brown butter. Penrose's signature. Adds caramel-nut depth.

Affogato Cocktail: espresso poured over vanilla gelato with a hit of Frangelico or Amaretto. Dessert-adjacent.

What to skip at any KL bar

Pre-batched bottled Espresso Martinis. Pre-mixed convenience-store cans (cans like "Espresso Martini Cocktail" sold at convenience stores in 2024-2025). Bars that use coffee syrup instead of fresh espresso. Anything described as "espresso-flavoured" rather than "espresso".

A real Espresso Martini takes 90 seconds to make. If your drink arrives within 30 seconds of ordering, ask if it was pre-batched.
