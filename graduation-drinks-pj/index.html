Graduation drinks in PJ.

A small-bar plan for the post-graduation celebration in Petaling Jaya. The mixed-generation family format, drinks for graduates and parents, NA programme, and how to brief the bar in advance.

Graduation is one of the calendar's underrated cocktail occasions. The format is unusual: the graduate is the centre, but the table is multi-generational (parents, grandparents, siblings, a partner or close friend). The drinks have to work for the 22-year-old and the 65-year-old simultaneously. A small craft cocktail bar handles this beautifully if you brief in advance.

The graduation drinks format

Group: typically 4-12 (graduate, immediate family, sometimes partner or two close friends).

Timing: after the ceremony, after the family dinner. Usually 9pm onward.

Duration: 90 minutes to 2.5 hours. Graduates often want to leave for a second-round with their own friends after.

The drinks order: a celebratory first round (champagne-based for everyone, or one custom drink for the graduate), a second round paced to the family conversation, an optional digestif.

What to order at a graduation

Pick drinks that read celebratory but not flashy. The parents and grandparents at the table tend to prefer classic over experimental.

French 75: gin, lemon, sugar, champagne. The classic graduation cocktail.

Champagne or prosecco by the glass: the universal celebratory move.

Negroni Sbagliato: Campari, sweet vermouth, prosecco. Lighter than the regular Negroni; works across generations.

Old Fashioned: for the parents and grandparents who appreciate a proper classic.

Bellini: peach and prosecco. Soft.

The custom graduate drink: ask the bartender to build a one-off cocktail for the graduate, briefed in advance. Their favourite spirit, a flavour they love, a flavour they hate. Named for the night, presented with a small recipe card.

Brief the bar in advance

WhatsApp 5-7 days ahead. Useful information:

  • Date and time you are arriving.
  • Group size and ages (roughly).
  • The graduate's name, favourite spirit, favourite flavour, hated flavour.
  • Any allergies, non-drinkers in the group.
  • Whether the graduate is bringing the cake or wants us to handle a small dessert moment.
  • Whether you want a custom drink built for the graduate.

We will reply within an hour with confirmation and any logistics.

For non-drinking family members

Graduation gatherings often include non-drinking parents, grandparents, or younger siblings. The NA programme:

  • NA French 75: NA gin + lemon + sugar + NA sparkling.
  • NA Bellini: peach + NA sparkling.
  • Coffee mocktails: Black Honey, Floral Mango, Peach Blossom.
  • Elderflower spritz.
  • NA Negroni: for the grandparent who used to drink one.
  • Sirap selasih: traditional Malaysian, often appreciated by older guests.

The graduation evening plan

Daytime: the ceremony, the photos.

Dinner, 7pm-9pm: family meal at a restaurant nearby.

Bar, 9pm-11pm: two or three rounds of celebratory drinks. The custom graduate drink lands on round one.

After 11pm: family heads home; graduate may continue with friends at a second venue (or stay for one more drink with us).
